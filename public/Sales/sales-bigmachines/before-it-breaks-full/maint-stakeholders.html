Business vocabulary
Glossary
stakeholder
parte interessada
Anyone with an interest in the outcome of a decision, whether they have formal authority or not.
Example: A real sale has many stakeholders, not just one decision maker.
bottom line
resultado final, lucro líquido
The final profit or financial result of a business, after all costs.
Example: "How will this improve our bottom line?"
status quo
o estado atual das coisas
The way things currently are, before any change.
Example: Frank's focus is protecting the status quo.
veto
vetar, poder de veto
The power to block a decision alone, even if everyone else agrees.
Example: Michael can veto on technical grounds alone.
approval chain
cadeia de aprovação
The sequence of people whose formal sign-off a deal needs before it's final.
Example: Frank isn't part of the approval chain, but he can still slow things down.
drive value
gerar valor
To create real, measurable benefit for the client, not just close a sale.
Example: Alex's job is to build relationships and drive value.
compliant
em conformidade
Following all the required rules, laws, or company policy.
Example: "Is this contract fair and compliant?"
navigate
administrar, lidar com
To move carefully through something complex, like a group of people with different interests. Not literal travel.
Example: Alex's power is the ability to navigate everyone else's.
in the room
presente, na conversa
Physically present in a meeting or conversation, able to speak for yourself.
Example: Emma fights for you when you're not in the room.
friction
atrito, resistência
Difficulty or resistance that slows a process down, often caused by disagreement.
Example: Frank has the power to create friction and doubt.
