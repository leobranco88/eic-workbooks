Was it happening?

Uma extensão da dedução no passado. Em vez de "aconteceu e terminou", às vezes você quer dizer "estava acontecendo naquele momento". Para isso, o inglês usa modal + have been + verbo-ing.

BONUS · NORTHWIND HQ
01
Something Was Happening Here
By Alex Morgan · Northwind HQ, 6am
Compressor room · Northwind HQ · early shift

Carlos and I walked into the compressor room at 6am. The floor near Unit 4 felt warm. A thin puddle of oil sat under the casing, still wet. Someone must have been working here, maybe just an hour before us.

Carlos checked the shift log. Nothing was written for the night. Nobody must have been watching this unit overnight. The sensor data showed a steady climb in temperature, all the way until 4am.

Emma joined us later with a different idea. The compressor might have been running on its own, just responding to demand from the line. Or steam from another pipe might have been leaking into the room, which could explain the warm floor without anyone actually there.

We checked the badge log next. It can't have been Carlos's night team. They can't have been working in this room. The doors stayed locked until 5:45am, and nobody signed in before that.

A Forma Contínua
Modal + have been + verbo-ing
~95%
must have been + -ing

Quase certeza de que algo estava em andamento.

"The floor was warm. Someone must have been working here."

~40 a 60%
might/could have been + -ing

Possibilidade de uma ação em progresso no passado.

"The compressor might have been running on its own."

~40 a 60%
may have been + -ing

Igual a "might have been". Mais formal, comum em relatórios.

"Steam may have been leaking from a nearby pipe."

~95%
can't have been + -ing

Praticamente impossível que a ação estivesse ocorrendo.

"They can't have been working there. The doors were locked."

A ideia central: a forma simples ("must have skipped") descreve uma ação completa. A forma contínua ("must have been skipping", quando faz sentido) descreve uma ação em progresso num momento específico do passado. É a mesma lógica do Present Continuous vs. Present Simple, só que aplicada à dedução.
ContextoSimples (ação completa)Contínua (em andamento)
O chão ainda estava quente e o óleo não tinha secado.Someone must have worked here.Someone must have been working here when the shift ended.
O sensor mostrou um aumento constante a noite toda, sem quedas.The unit must have overheated.The unit must have been overheating for hours before anyone noticed.
O registro de crachá mostra as portas trancadas a noite toda.It can't have been Carlos's team.They can't have been working there. The doors were locked.
Como escolher
Pistas de contexto que pedem a forma contínua: when I arrived, at that moment, still, for two hours, on and off. Pistas que pedem a forma simples: already, by the time, ou evidência de um resultado já finalizado (peça já trocada, relatório já assinado, tarefa já concluída).
Simples vs. contínua

You walk into the compressor room and find Carlos's toolbox open, a wrench still lying across the casing, and the panel light blinking mid-cycle.

He must have been repairing the unit when something interrupted him.

Por que a forma contínua: a ferramenta ficou largada no meio da tarefa e a luz do painel ainda pisca no meio do ciclo. As evidências apontam para uma ação interrompida, em andamento, não uma ação já concluída.

Impossibilidade contínua

Emma says the compressor was running fine all afternoon, but the maintenance log shows the whole plant lost power from 1pm to 3pm.

It can't have been running all afternoon. There was no power for two hours.

Por que "can't have been running": a evidência (a falta de energia) torna fisicamente impossível a ação contínua alegada.
