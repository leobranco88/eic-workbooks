Getting Past Sandra
1 · Research 2 · Outreach 3 · First Contact 4 · Technical Proof 5 · Building an Ally 6 · Checking In 7 · Money & Terms 8 · The Finish Line
Prisma Coatings, front desk. 9:14 AM.
Scene Context
Diego has never met anyone at Prisma Coatings in person. Sandra runs the front desk and decides who gets past it. She doesn't know him yet, and she has no reason to trust him.
Scene Objectives
  • Get past the front desk, one way or another.
  • Keep the relationship with Sandra intact, whatever happens.
  • Notice which choice gives her a reason to say yes.
Useful Language
"I hope this isn't out of the blue, but I wanted to stop by."Espero que não seja do nada, mas eu queria dar uma passada aqui.
"I know I don't have an appointment, I should have called ahead."Eu sei que não tenho hora marcada, eu deveria ter ligado antes.
"Would it be possible to grab five minutes if he's free?"Seria possível conseguir cinco minutinhos, se ele estiver livre?
"No worries at all if now isn't a good time."Sem problema nenhum se agora não for uma boa hora.
Talk About It
What makes a gatekeeper decide to let someone through?
What is the difference between being persistent and being pushy?
Why does naming a colleague change how a request sounds?
Discuss with your partner or teacher before moving on.
