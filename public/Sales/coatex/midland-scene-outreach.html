Before Anyone Answers
These are the steps most sales reps are told to follow before reaching out to a new company. Mark what you actually do.
Estes são os passos que geralmente se recomenda seguir antes de abordar uma empresa nova. Marque o que você realmente faz.
Step 1
Research the company before writing anything: what they make, who their clients are, what changed recently.
Pesquisar a empresa antes de escrever qualquer coisa.
Step 2
Find out who actually decides, not just who answers the phone.
Descobrir quem realmente decide, não só quem atende o telefone.
Step 3
Look for a warm introduction before trying a cold one.
Procurar uma apresentação por indicação antes de tentar o contato frio.
Step 4
Prepare one specific reason why this company, and not any company.
Preparar um motivo específico pra essa empresa, e não pra qualquer empresa.
Step 5
Decide the channel on purpose: email, phone, message, or showing up.
Escolher o canal de propósito: e-mail, telefone, mensagem, ou aparecer pessoalmente.
Step 6
Plan the follow-up before you send the first message.
Planejar o retorno antes mesmo de mandar a primeira mensagem.

Scene Context
Diego found Prisma Coatings on a list of paint manufacturers in Jundiaí. Nobody there knows him. He has one message to earn a reply, and Fernando wants movement on this account by Friday.
Scene Objectives
  • Earn a reply, not just send a message.
  • Show you did your homework on this specific company.
  • Make the next step small and easy to say yes to.
