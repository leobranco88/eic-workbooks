Flagging It to Procurement
Prisma Coatings, Procurement Office. 9:15 AM.
Scene Context
Carlos told Amauri that Thinner A is critical, only a couple of days of stock left. Now Amauri has to walk it over to Patrícia in Procurement before it becomes a real production problem.
Scene Objectives
  • Flag the problem with a real number, not a guess.
  • Answer a follow-up question about timing clearly.
  • Show urgency without sounding like you are panicking.
