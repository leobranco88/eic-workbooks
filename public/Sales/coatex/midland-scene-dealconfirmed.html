Vocabulary · Chat vs. Email
We're in.
"Diego! We're in."
Formal alternativesThe deal has been confirmed. / The order has been approved.
Sent it your way.
"Just sent the PO your way, check your inbox."
Formal alternativesI have just sent it to you. / Please find it attached.
Locked in
"Locked in at 8% and Net 45, like we agreed."
Formal alternativesFinalized at. / Confirmed at. / Agreed upon at.
Land at
"Landed at 8%, right at the ceiling you gave me."
Formal alternativesThe final figure reached. / We settled on. / The outcome was.
Ceiling
"Right at the ceiling you gave me."
Formal alternativesThe approved limit. / The maximum authorized rate. / The upper boundary.
We got it.
"Fernando, we got it. Prisma just confirmed."
Formal alternativesWe have secured the order. / The deal has closed successfully.
