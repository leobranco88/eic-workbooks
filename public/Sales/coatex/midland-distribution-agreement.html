DA-2241In Force
DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT
Between Prisma Coatings ("Buyer") and Coatex Distribuidora ("Distributor")
Art. 1
Parties and Term

This Agreement takes effect on the date of signature and remains in force for an initial term of twelve months, renewing automatically unless either party gives sixty days written notice before the renewal date.

Art. 2
Territory and Exclusivity

Coatex is authorized to supply the agreed product line to Prisma within the São Paulo metro region. This Agreement is non-exclusive: Prisma remains free to buy equivalent products from other suppliers, and Coatex remains free to sell to other clients in the same territory.

Art. 3
Pricing and Payment Terms

Unit pricing, discounts, and payment terms are not fixed by this Agreement. They are set individually on each Purchase Order. Standard payment terms are Net 30 unless a different term is confirmed in writing on the Purchase Order itself.

Art. 4
Order Process

Every purchase under this Agreement is placed through a Purchase Order referencing DA-2241. Standard order conditions, including delivery, custom requests, storage, and cancellation, are governed by Exhibit C, attached to and forming part of this Agreement.

Art. 5
Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with sixty days written notice. Either party may terminate immediately for a material breach that is not corrected within fifteen days of written notice.

Art. 6
Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Brazil. Any dispute arising from it is subject to the courts of the state of São Paulo.
