Stage 07 · Money & Terms
Same Price. Two Different Filters.
Diego Ramos
Sales Rep · Coatex Distribuidora
Diego already has Beatriz on his side. Now the conversation moves to the two people who actually control the paperwork: Procurement and the Plant Director. He wants to prove he can sell to a technical, multi-layered buying committee, not just a single decision maker. Patricia and Marcos will push back from two different directions, in the same week.
Competitor Watch
Ferro & Cia Química
Regional distributor
-15%
Undercuts on price by cutting technical support. Still holds local stock, so delivery risk is low. This is the quote Patricia has on her desk right now.
Dong Fang Importacao
Direct China import trading
-35%
Prices this low because it ships in bulk from China. 45 to 60 day lead time, inconsistent batch quality, no technical support. Not on the table yet, but it is what Marcos is watching for next quarter.
Patricia Nunes
Procurement Manager
She wants the best commercial terms: price, payment conditions, and delivery guarantees.
Marcos Vieira
Plant Director · Economic Buyer
He wants a clear return on investment and a budget that does not go over plan this quarter.
Document 07 Procurement

The competitor quote

Patricia already knows Beatriz likes the coating. That is not her problem. Her problem is a second quote on her desk, for what looks like the same product, at a lower price.

Patricia Nunes
I have a quote from Ferro & Cia, fifteen percent lower, for what looks like the same coating. Why should I pay more for the same chemical?
Your move, before you read Diego's answer
If the coating itself is basically interchangeable, what can Diego still defend? What does Coatex actually sell that a cheaper competitor might not?
Diego
I understand, and on paper the specs look close. But that quote does not include what happens after the order ships. Coatex guarantees delivery inside seventy two hours because we hold local stock, the other supplier imports on request. If a batch is delayed, that is a stopped production line, not just a late invoice. I am not asking you to match the price, I am asking you to compare the total cost of ownership, including what is not written on either quote.
Why this works
Diego does not argue about the chemical. He moves the comparison to the layer Coatex actually controls: logistics reliability and technical support. He gives Patricia a new number for her spreadsheet instead of asking her to ignore the one she already has.
Useful Language
Price vs. value
Preco vs. valor: comparar o custo do momento com o beneficio total.
Total cost of ownership
Custo total de propriedade: tudo que o produto custa ao longo do tempo, nao so o preco de compra.
Match the price / match the value
Igualar o preco / igualar o valor: duas estrategias diferentes diante de uma cotacao mais barata.
What's not in the quote
O que nao esta na cotacao: os custos ou riscos escondidos que o preco baixo nao mostra.
Document 08 Economic Buyer

The number on his budget line

Marcos does not care about adhesion tests or delivery windows. He cares about one thing: does this fit inside the budget he already reported to his own director, this quarter.

Marcos Vieira
Beatriz likes the product. Patricia says the terms are fine. I just need to know this does not go over what I already approved.
Your move, before you read Diego's answer
Diego already used the logistics argument with Patricia. Will the same argument work on Marcos? What does his filter actually respond to?
Diego
The unit price sits inside the range Patricia already reviewed, so it does not touch your budget line this quarter. And every production stop your plant has had this year seems to trace back to a coating delay from your current supplier. One stopped shift costs more than a full year of the difference we are talking about. This is not a bigger number, it is a safer one.
Why this works
Same facts, different filter. Diego does not repeat the logistics story he told Patricia, he translates it into what Marcos actually tracks: budget lines and production stops.
Useful Language
Go over budget / stay inside budget
Estourar o orcamento / ficar dentro do orcamento.
Budget line
Linha de orcamento: a categoria especifica de gasto que alguem controla e reporta.
Trace back to
Ter origem em, remontar a: encontrar a causa real de um problema.
Stopped shift / stopped line
Turno parado / linha parada: producao interrompida.
The pattern behind both documents

Same facts. Different filter.

Patricia and Marcos received the same underlying information: Coatex holds local stock, and stock-outs from the current supplier have already cost production time. Diego did not lie to either of them, and he did not repeat himself either. He chose what to foreground based on WHO he was talking to, not just WHAT the facts were.

To Patricia (Procurement)
The fact becomes a commercial guarantee: reliable delivery, written into the terms she negotiates.
To Marcos (Economic Buyer)
The same fact becomes a budget argument: avoided cost, measured against his own numbers.
Practice: Which filter is this?
Read the whole sentence first. Decide if it sounds like something Patricia would say or something Marcos would say, then click the gap to check.
