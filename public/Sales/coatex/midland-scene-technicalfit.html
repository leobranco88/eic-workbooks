Building an Ally · Confirming the Fit

Does It Actually Work Here?

AdhereShield 450 is not a commodity resin. Before Beatriz puts her name behind it internally, she wants Coatex's own technical team to confirm it fits Prisma's exact production line, not just the data sheet.

1The Email

Beatriz skips Diego entirely on this one. Formulation questions go to formulation people.

Formulation fit check, AdhereShield 450
FromBeatriz Lima <beatriz.lima@prismacoatings.com.br>
ToJuliana Prado <juliana.prado@coatexdistribuidora.com.br>

Hi Juliana,

Diego mentioned you handle technical service on your side, so I wanted to go straight to the source before this goes any further.

Our line runs at a fixed speed, which means anything we apply needs to cure within 90 seconds at 60°C. We are coating a porous cast surface, not the smooth panels most data sheets assume. I need to know if AdhereShield 450 actually holds up here, not just on paper.

Could you confirm compatibility, or tell me what would need to change?

Best,
Beatriz Lima
Formulation Chemist, R&D, Prisma Coatings

Reply
RE: Formulation fit check, AdhereShield 450
FromJuliana Prado <juliana.prado@coatexdistribuidora.com.br>
ToBeatriz Lima <beatriz.lima@prismacoatings.com.br>

Hi Beatriz,

Good question, and the honest answer is not out of the box. Standard AdhereShield 450 cures in around 140 seconds at 60°C, that would not fit your line speed.

Porosity is not the issue, the resin holds adhesion well on cast surfaces. The cure window is. We can adjust the catalyst ratio to bring that down to 85 seconds without affecting adhesion strength, that is a standard formulation adjustment on our end, not a new product.

I have attached the full compatibility report with the adjusted parameters. Let me know if your team wants to run a trial batch before Diego finalizes anything.

Best,
Juliana Prado
Technical Service Engineer, Coatex Distribuidora
