The Supply Agreement, and what it quietly leaves out

Prisma Coatings signed a standard Supply Agreement with Coatex, and assumed it meant Coatex handles everything. 1The Exhibit

Every clause below is real for Prisma's account. Bruno reads from this before every call, not from memory.

DA-2241Rev. 02
EXHIBIT C
Conditions Applicable to Standard Supply Orders
Attached to and forming part of Distribution Agreement DA-2241 · Rev. 02 · Page 1 of 2

Where this Exhibit conflicts with the Purchase Order confirmation, the Purchase Order confirmation takes precedence.

C.1
Scope of the Standard Order

The Purchase Order confirmation is the list. It states the exact products, colors, and quantities Coatex will deliver, standard packaging, and one Certificate of Analysis per batch. Anything requested beyond what is on the confirmed PO is quoted separately, and requires its own written confirmation before it is added to the order.

C.2
Freight and Delivery

Standard delivery is FOB Destination within the São Paulo metro region, on the lead time confirmed at order placement. Delivery outside this radius, split shipments, or any delivery date moved earlier than confirmed at the client's request is billed as a separate freight charge.

C.3
Custom Requests

Custom color matching outside the standard catalog, private label packaging, and additional Certificates of Analysis beyond the one per batch are billable and require written approval before production starts. Verbal requests during a visit are not enough to change the order.

C.4
Client-Caused Delay or Error

Coatex is not obliged to absorb delays caused by the client's site not being ready to receive delivery, incorrect quantities or colors ordered due to an error on the client's own purchase order, or deliveries rejected because of a schedule change on the client's side. Coatex may still assist, quoted as a new order.

C.5
Storage and Shelf Life

Shelf life is stated on the Certificate of Analysis and the product label. Once product leaves the Coatex warehouse, responsibility for proper storage, including temperature and humidity, transfers to the client. Product that degrades due to improper storage after delivery is not covered.

C.6
Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled at no cost prior to the start of production. Once a batch has entered production, cancellation is billable for the cost of materials and labor already committed.

2Dois exemplos resolvidos

Veja o raciocínio antes de tentar sozinho.

Included

Patricia checks the delivered order. It matches the confirmed PO exactly: standard packaging, standard region, and the batch Certificate of Analysis is attached.

Por quê "Included": a pista é standard packaging, standard region, Certificate of Analysis. Isso não é combinado nem exceção, é exatamente o que a PO confirmada já prometia. Sob C.1, nada disso gera fatura extra.

Not Covered

Jonas opens the drums and finds the wrong color. Patricia's team had listed an outdated product code on their own purchase order.

Por quê "Not Covered": a pista é outdated product code vindo do lado do cliente. Sob C.4, um erro na própria PO do cliente não é responsabilidade da Coatex. Bruno ainda pode ajudar, mas como um pedido novo, com orçamento.
