Before You Ever Say Hello

Every deal starts with a message nobody asked for. This is the language Diego needs before he ever writes an email, sends a WhatsApp, or picks up the phone. General enough to use with anyone, specific enough to sound like he did his homework.

Functional Phrases

Nothing here is industry-specific. These are the moves that work whether Diego is prospecting a paint company or anyone else. The kind of English that transfers.

Breaking the Ice
"I hope this isn't out of the blue, but I wanted to reach out."
"I'll keep this short, just a quick note about..."
Referencing Research
"I noticed your team recently expanded the production line."
"I came across your work on [project] and thought I'd reach out."
Building Credibility Fast
"We've worked with a few companies in your segment before."
"Happy to share a short example if that's useful."
Asking for Time
"Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call this week?"
"No pressure at all, even a short reply works for now."
Handling Pushback
"No problem at all, mind if I check back in a quarter?"
"Totally understand. Can I send something for later?"
Closing With a Next Step
"I'll follow up next Tuesday if that works for you."
"I'll send a calendar invite so it's easy to lock in."
Technical Glossary

These come up once Diego is past the opening line, in specs, in procurement talk, in the numbers. They don't belong in a cold email, but he needs to recognize them fast when they show up.

Spec Sheet
Ficha técnica com as especificações do produto.
"Ask for the spec sheet before sending a quote."
Lead Time
Prazo entre o pedido e a entrega.
"Our lead time is six weeks from order confirmation."
Adhesion Promoter
Produto que melhora a aderência da tinta em superfícies difíceis.
"This resin needs an adhesion promoter on porous surfaces."
Procurement Cycle
O processo interno de compras, do pedido até a aprovação final.
"Every account goes through the same procurement cycle before a PO is issued."
Case Study
Um exemplo real de um cliente parecido, usado como prova social.
"Can I send a quick case study from a similar project?"
Value Proposition
A promessa central: por que essa solução vale a pena.
"Our value proposition is simple: fewer callbacks, less downtime."
