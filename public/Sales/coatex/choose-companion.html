Choose Your Companion

This character will stay with you through the course. It will show up with quick tips, questions to think about before a scene, reminders to practice vocabulary you have saved, and a word of encouragement before a big document.

How this works. Pick the coaching style that fits how you like to learn, not how you look. All four give you the same kind of help at the same moments, they just say it differently. You can change your choice at any time from the course menu.
The Strategist
Calm · Deliberate
Asks you a question before giving you an answer.
The Closer
Direct · High Energy
Short messages, clear pushes, quick to celebrate a win.
The Mentor
Patient · Steady
Keeps circling back to vocabulary you have not practiced yet.
The Analyst
Sharp · Precise
Points out patterns and puts a number behind the advice.
You can change this later from the course menu.
