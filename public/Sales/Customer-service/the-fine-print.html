The Fine Print — what's actually covered

Not every complaint is Altiva's fault — and not every client agrees with that. Renata's job includes reading each case against the company's actual policy, not just what feels fair in the moment. Read the policy once, then judge five real cases the way she would.

Renata Lima · Customer Success, Altiva Biosolutions
01The Policy

Leia uma vez com calma — as expressões destacadas em neon são as que decidem cada caso lá embaixo.

Altiva Biosolutions
Internal Policy · Shipping Damage & Claims
Section 4: Damage Claims and Liability

4.1 — Transit Damage

Damage is covered under the policy when it is reported within 5 business days of delivery, with photographs taken before the delivery confirmation is signed. Claims reported after this window, or without photographic evidence, are considered excluded from coverage.

4.2 — Storage and Handling

Once a shipment is accepted in good condition, any damage caused by improper storage (excessive heat, direct sunlight, exposure to moisture) falls outside our responsibility. Altiva is not liable for product that degrades after delivery due to the client's own handling.

4.3 — Internal Errors

When a shipment error originates on Altiva's side — incorrect product, incorrect concentration, or a packing mistake at our facility — Altiva is at fault regardless of how the client discovers it, and the claim meets the conditions for a claim automatically.

4.4 — Client Ordering Errors

Requests to return correctly delivered product due to over-ordering, wrong product selection, or changed requirements on the client's part are not covered. These situations are the client's responsibility, not Altiva's.

In plain English

For example

Teacher's Note
"Fall" here has nothing to do with falling down. In English, things can "fall under" a category (belong to it) or "fall outside" a category (not belong to it) — like a metaphorical box something drops into or misses entirely. "Fall" aqui não tem nada a ver com cair. Em inglês, algo pode "fall under" uma categoria (pertencer a ela) ou "fall outside" uma categoria (não pertencer) — como se fosse uma caixa metafórica onde a coisa cai dentro ou cai fora.
"Meet" doesn't always mean "to meet someone" (conhecer). With words like condition, requirement, deadline, expectation, "meet" means to satisfy or fulfill something. "Meet the conditions" = atender/cumprir as condições — not "conhecer" as condições. "Meet" nem sempre significa "conhecer alguém". Com palavras como "condition, requirement, deadline, expectation", "meet" significa satisfazer ou cumprir algo.
Mission: try building your own sentence with each verb — one with "fall under/outside," one with "meet" (not about people). Say them out loud before moving to the cases below.

Case 2
A different client noticed a small dent on one drum, but didn't mention it until 12 days later, in an unrelated email. No photos exist from the day of delivery.
From: Marcos Andrade <compras@frutamax.com.br>
To: Renata Lima <renata@altivabio.com>
Date: 12 days after delivery
Re: Invoice question / small issue
Hi Renata, hope all is well. While we were going through this month's invoices I remembered — one of the drums from that last shipment had a small dent on the side. Not a big deal, just flagging it. Let me know if that changes anything on our end.

No attachment · no photo included with this email
Not Covered — under 4.1, this is well past the 5-business-day window, and there's no photographic evidence from arrival.
How Renata replies to Marcos
"Thanks for flagging this, Marcos. Since it's been over two weeks and we don't have photos from delivery day, I'm not able to process this as a transit claim — but happy to talk through it if you'd like."
Case 3
Vale Cítrico accepted a shipment in good condition, then left the drums outside in direct sunlight for two weeks. The enzyme inside had degraded by the time they used it.
Photo submitted by the client, taken two weeks after delivery — it shows the drums outside in direct sun, but doesn't prove anything about their condition during transit.
Not Covered — under 4.2, this is improper storage after an accepted delivery, not a transit issue. It falls outside Altiva's responsibility.
How Renata replies
"I appreciate you sending photos, but since the drums were stored outside after delivery, this falls outside what our shipping policy covers. Happy to share some storage guidelines going forward."
