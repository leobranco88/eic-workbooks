The Leaking Drums — handling a damaged shipment

Renata Lima is the Customer Success Manager at Altiva Biosolutions, which supplies industrial enzymes to food and beverage producers. When a shipment of pectinase enzyme arrives at a client's facility with damaged drums, it's Renata who has to handle the call — without losing the account.

Renata Lima · Customer Success, Altiva Biosolutions Client: Vale Cítrico — orange juice producer

Describing the Damage
"We received a punctured drum in this shipment, plus a second drum with a leak during transit that left enzyme all over the pallet. Another drum had a broken seal, so we have to treat it as a contaminated batch; it's unfit for use in our juice line. We photographed the visible damage the moment the truck arrived."
Bruno · Quality Manager, Vale Cítrico
Resolving the Complaint
"Once Bruno sent photos, it took us five minutes to inspect the damage and confirm it was real. Given his production schedule, I offered to expedite the shipment and get a replacement shipment out within 48 hours. We also agreed to issue a credit for the two drums we lost, and we'll file a claim with the carrier ourselves. We're already changing the packaging to prevent this from happening again."
Renata Lima · Customer Success, Altiva Biosolutions

02Build the Call: Handling the Complaint

Bruno liga preocupado com o cronograma de produção de suco. Em cada momento, escolha a frase que a Renata diria.

Bruno · Vale Cítrico
"Two of the five drums arrived punctured. We're running the juice line tomorrow morning and now I'm short on enzyme."
What does Renata say?
Asking for photos is the fastest way to confirm the damage and move straight to a solution — not an excuse, not a shrug.
Bruno · Vale Cítrico
"Sent them. But photos don't fix my production schedule for tomorrow."
What does Renata say?
This directly answers his real concern — the production schedule — instead of talking about the carrier claim, which isn't his problem.
Bruno · Vale Cítrico
"And what about the two drums we lost completely?"
What does Renata say?
A same-day credit is concrete and immediate — it closes the financial question instead of leaving it open.
Bruno · Vale Cítrico
"Okay. I just don't want this happening every quarter."
How does Renata close the call?
A concrete process change — not a promise in the abstract — is what actually rebuilds confidence after a repeated problem.
