01
Stock Alert
Operations ManagerProcurement Manager
The ERP system detects that stock levels of compound HX-44 have fallen below the reorder point. The system generates an automatic stock alert. The Operations Manager confirms the urgency and contacts Procurement.
Key document
Stock Alert / Reorder Request
An internal system notification or form that triggers the procurement process. It includes the item code, current quantity, minimum required level, and the date by which stock must be replenished.
Operations Manager → Procurement Manager
"Line 3 consumption is running 20% above forecast. We currently have 800 units of HX-44 — that is 11 days of cover at current rate. We need to reorder immediately or we risk a stockout before the end of the month."
Watch your language
run out of — not finish
"We are going to run out of HX-44 by the 20th." — Not: finish the HX-44 or end the stock.
Quick check
The system shows 800 units remaining. At current consumption, that is 11 days of cover.
What does "days of cover" mean here?
02
Supplier Selection & RFQ
Procurement ManagerAccount Manager
Procurement reviews the approved supplier list and selects two or three candidates. They prepare and send a formal RFQ — specifying the item, quantity, required lead time, Incoterm, and payment terms. This is not an order — it is a request for information.
Key document
Request for Quotation (RFQ)
A formal document sent to suppliers asking for price, lead time, MOQ, and delivery conditions. A well-written RFQ forces all suppliers to quote the same thing — making comparison fair and fast.
Procurement Manager → Supplier (Hua Li Components)
"Dear Mr. Chen, please find attached our RFQ #BR-2024-088 for 2,000 units of compound HX-44. We require your quotation by 10 November including unit price, MOQ, lead time from PO confirmation, and payment terms. Please quote CIF Santos."
Watch your language
send an RFQ — not open or do
"We sent an RFQ to three suppliers yesterday." — Not: we opened an RFQ or we did an RFQ.
Quick check
The RFQ specifies CIF Santos as the required Incoterm.
If the RFQ says "quote CIF Santos," who pays for shipping and insurance?
03
Quotation & Comparison
Procurement ManagerAccount Manager
Suppliers respond with their quotations. Procurement builds a comparison table — evaluating price, lead time, MOQ, payment terms, and quality certifications. The cheapest option is not always the best: a lower price with a 60-day lead time may cost more than a higher price delivered in 30 days.
Key document
Quotation / Supplier Comparison Table
The supplier's formal response to the RFQ — with price, lead time, and conditions. The comparison table puts all quotes side by side so Procurement can make a fair, documented decision.
Procurement Manager → Operations Manager
"Hua Li is quoting USD 3.20/unit with a 30-day lead time. GlobalParts is at USD 2.95 but lead time is 55 days and they do not hold ISO 9001. Given our current days of cover, I recommend Hua Li — the price difference does not justify the risk."
Watch your language
compare quotes — not prices alone
Always evaluate total cost of ownership — price + lead time + quality risk + payment terms. The cheapest unit price often becomes the most expensive decision. / Sempre avalie o custo total, não só o preço unitário.
Quick check
GlobalParts quoted USD 2.95/unit but does not hold ISO 9001 certification.
Why does the absence of ISO 9001 matter in a procurement decision?
04
Internal Approval
Procurement ManagerFinance / APManagement
Before issuing a Purchase Order, Procurement needs internal sign-off. For orders above a certain value — typically defined in the company's procurement policy — Finance and Management must approve. This step protects the company from unauthorised spending.
Key document
Purchase Requisition / Approval Form
An internal document requesting authorisation to spend. It includes the supplier chosen, the price, the justification, and the budget code. Without approval, no PO can be issued.
Procurement Manager → Finance
"I am submitting the purchase requisition for 2,000 units of HX-44 from Hua Li Components at USD 3.20/unit — total value USD 6,400. This is within my approval threshold but I need Finance to confirm budget availability before I raise the PO."
Watch your language
raise a PO — not open or create
"I will raise the PO as soon as Finance approves the budget." — Not: open the PO or create the PO. In procurement English, you raise or issue a Purchase Order.
Quick check
This order is within my approval threshold — I can approve it without going to the director.
What is an "approval threshold" in a procurement context?
05
Purchase Order
Procurement ManagerAccount Manager
With approval confirmed, Procurement issues the Purchase Order. The PO is a legal commitment — it locks in the price, quantity, delivery date, and payment terms. The supplier must formally acknowledge and accept the PO before production begins. Any change after this point requires a formal amendment.
Key document
Purchase Order (PO)
The formal commitment from buyer to supplier. Once accepted by the supplier, it is legally binding on both sides. It defines exactly what will be delivered, when, where, and at what price.
Procurement Manager → Hua Li Account Manager
"Please find attached Purchase Order #BR-2024-0091 for 2,000 units of HX-44 at USD 3.20/unit CIF Santos, total USD 6,400. Delivery is required by 14 December 2024. Please confirm receipt and acceptance of this PO by return and advise your expected ship date."
Watch your language
confirm receipt — not confirm you received
"Please confirm receipt of this PO." — Not: confirm that you received. In business English, "confirm receipt" is the standard formal phrase for acknowledging a document.
Quick check
Any change after the PO is issued requires a formal amendment.
The delivery date in the PO is 14 December, but the supplier calls and verbally agrees to extend it to 5 January. Is this enough?
06
Production & Shipment
Sales EngineerQuality ManagerLogistics Coordinator
The supplier produces the goods according to the agreed specification. Before shipping, the Quality Manager runs an outgoing inspection and issues a certificate of conformance. The Logistics Coordinator books freight, prepares shipping documents, and confirms the ETD.
Key document
Certificate of Conformance + Shipping Documents
The certificate of conformance confirms the goods meet the spec. The shipping documents — Bill of Lading, packing list, commercial invoice — are required for customs clearance at the destination port.
Hua Li Logistics Coordinator → Althea Procurement
"All 2,000 units of HX-44 passed outgoing inspection against your specification Rev.3. We have confirmed vessel booking for 18 November, ETD Shanghai. Bill of Lading and packing list will follow within 24 hours of departure. ETA Santos: 10 December."
Watch your language
ETD vs ETA
ETD (Estimated Time of Departure) = when the ship leaves. ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival) = when it arrives. Confusing them causes real planning errors. / ETD = quando o navio parte. ETA = quando chega. Confundi-los causa erros reais de planejamento.
Quick check
The Bill of Lading will be sent within 24 hours of departure.
Why is the Bill of Lading critical for the buyer?
07
Customs & Receiving
Procurement ManagerOperations / Warehouse
The shipment arrives at Santos port. The freight forwarder handles customs clearance using the Bill of Lading, commercial invoice, and packing list. Once cleared, goods are transported to Althea's warehouse. The warehouse team runs an incoming inspection — checking quantity, packaging, and visible condition before issuing a goods receipt.
Key document
Goods Receipt Note (GRN)
An internal document confirming that goods were physically received, in the correct quantity and acceptable condition. The GRN is required by Finance before processing any invoice from the supplier.
Warehouse / Operations → Procurement
"The shipment arrived at our warehouse this morning. Quantity confirmed: 2,000 units of HX-44, batch #HX-2024-114. Packaging intact. We have issued the goods receipt and sent a copy to Finance. The goods are now available for production."
Watch your language
incoming inspection — not receiving inspection
The standard term is incoming inspection (also: incoming quality control / IQC). Not: receiving inspection. This is what happens when goods arrive — a check before they go into stock. / O termo padrão é incoming inspection ou IQC.
Quick check
Finance will not process the invoice until they receive the goods receipt note.
What is the main purpose of a goods receipt note in the procurement process?
08
Invoice & Payment
Finance / APAccount Manager
The supplier raises the invoice after the goods are delivered and accepted. Finance performs a three-way match — checking that the invoice, the Purchase Order, and the goods receipt all agree on quantity, price, and supplier details. If everything matches, payment is released within the agreed payment terms.
Key document
Invoice + Three-Way Match
The supplier's payment request. Finance will only process it after matching it against the PO (what was ordered) and the GRN (what was received). Any discrepancy — wrong price, wrong quantity, missing PO number — puts the invoice on hold.
Finance / Accounts Payable → Hua Li Account Manager
"This invoice cannot be processed. The unit price on the invoice is USD 3.25 but PO #BR-2024-0091 shows USD 3.20. Please resubmit with the correct amount or send a credit note for the difference. Payment will be released within Net 60 from receipt of the corrected invoice."
Watch your language
release payment — not liberate or free
"Finance will release payment once the three-way match is complete." — Not: liberate the payment. "Release" is the standard verb for authorising a payment to go through. / "Release" é o verbo padrão para autorizar um pagamento.
Quick check
Finance performs a three-way match before releasing any payment.
What are the three documents in a "three-way match"?
