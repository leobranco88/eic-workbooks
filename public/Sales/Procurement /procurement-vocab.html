Documents
Request for Quotation (RFQ)
Pedido de Cotação
Context clue
Internal procurement memo
Before issuing a Purchase Order, the procurement team sent a Request for Quotation (RFQ) to three potential suppliers to compare prices and lead times.
A formal document sent by a buyer to one or more suppliers asking them to provide a price and delivery conditions for specific goods or services. It is not an order — it is a request for information.
Em português: Documento formal enviado pelo comprador a um ou mais fornecedores solicitando preço e condições de entrega para produtos ou serviços específicos. Não é um pedido — é uma solicitação de informação.
Real example
"Dear Mr. Chen, please find attached our RFQ #BR-2024-088 for 500 units of sensor component SKU-7712. We kindly request your quotation by 15 November, including unit price, MOQ, lead time, and payment terms."
Buyer email to supplier
Documents
Purchase Order (PO)
Ordem de Compra
Context clue
Procurement team meeting notes
Once the supplier confirmed the price and delivery date, we issued a Purchase Order (PO) to formally commit to the transaction.
A formal document issued by the buyer to confirm a purchase. Unlike an RFQ, a PO is a commitment — it locks in price, quantity, delivery date, and payment terms. The supplier is legally entitled to produce and invoice based on an accepted PO.
Em português: Documento formal emitido pelo comprador para confirmar uma compra. Diferente do RFQ, o PO é um compromisso — ele define preço, quantidade, prazo de entrega e condições de pagamento. O fornecedor tem direito legal de produzir e emitir fatura com base em um PO aceito.
Real example
"Please find attached Purchase Order #BR-2024-0091 for 500 units of SKU-7712 at USD 4.80 per unit, CIF SantosCost, Insurance, Freight — seller pays shipping and insurance to Santos port. / O vendedor paga pelo frete e seguro até o porto de Santos.. Delivery is required by 10 January 2025. Please confirm receipt and acceptance of this PO by return email."
Buyer email to supplier
Documents
Purchase Agreement
Contrato de Fornecimento
Context clue
Supplier relationship review
After three years of using individual POs for every order, the company decided to sign a Purchase Agreement with their main raw material supplier to reduce administrative burden and lock in pricing.
A formal contract that governs a longer-term supply relationship. While a PO covers a single transaction, a Purchase Agreement sets the rules for all transactions over a defined period — including penalty conditions, warranty obligations, and what happens when something goes wrong.
Em português: Contrato formal que rege um relacionamento de fornecimento de longo prazo. Enquanto o PO cobre uma única transação, o Purchase Agreement define as regras para todas as transações em um período determinado — incluindo condições de penalidade, obrigações de garantia e o que acontece quando algo dá errado.
Real example
"This Agreement sets forth the terms and conditions under which Supplier shall supply Goods to Buyer during the Term. Individual Purchase Orders issued hereunder are subject to the terms of this Agreement."
Purchase Agreement, Clause 1.1

Lead Time

Context clue
Supply planning email
The supplier in Taiwan quoted a Lead Time of 45 days from PO confirmation, which means we need to place orders in October to receive goods before the December shutdown.
The total time between placing an order and receiving the goods. It includes production time, quality checks, and shipping. Critical for planning and stock management — a long lead time means you need to order earlier.
Em português: O tempo total entre o momento do pedido e o recebimento dos produtos. Inclui tempo de produção, controles de qualidade e transporte. Crítico para planejamento e gestão de estoque — um lead time longo significa que é preciso pedir com mais antecedência.

"Please include the following in your quotation: unit price (CIF SantosCost, Insurance, Freight — seller pays shipping and insurance to Santos port. / O vendedor paga pelo frete e seguro até o porto de Santos.), minimum order quantity (MOQ), lead time from PO confirmation, and payment terms."

Operations
MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)
Quantidade Mínima de Pedido
Context clue
Supplier evaluation notes
The supplier confirmed a price of USD 3.20 per unit, but the MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) is 1,000 units — three times more than our monthly demand.
The smallest quantity a supplier is willing to sell in a single order. If your demand is below the MOQ, you either order more than you need or find an alternative supplier. MOQ directly affects your inventory and cash flow.
Em português: A menor quantidade que um fornecedor está disposto a vender em um único pedido. Se sua demanda está abaixo do MOQ, você pede mais do que precisa ou busca um fornecedor alternativo. O MOQ afeta diretamente o estoque e o fluxo de caixa.
Real example
"Please confirm the following: unit price, MOQ, lead time from PO confirmation, and whether you can offer a reduced MOQ for a trial order of 200 units."
RFQ email — buyer to supplier

Condições de Pagamento

Finance team discussion
Our standard Payment Terms are Net 60 from invoice date, but this supplier is requesting 30% upfront and 70% against Bill of LadingA legal shipping document (B/LBill of Lading — a legal shipping document issued by the carrier confirming receipt of goods. / Conhecimento de embarque — documento legal de transporte.) issued by the carrier confirming receipt of goods and their destination. / Documento legal de transporte (B/LBill of Lading — a legal shipping document issued by the carrier confirming receipt of goods. / Conhecimento de embarque — documento legal de transporte.) que confirma o recebimento e destino dos produtos..
The agreed conditions for when and how the buyer will pay the supplier. Common terms include Net 30 (payment within 30 days of invoice), Net 60, or a percentage upfront with the balance on delivery. These terms directly affect cash flow for both sides.

Contract Language
Time is of the essence
O prazo é essencial / fundamental
Context clue
Purchase Agreement, Clause 4.2
The clause read: "Delivery shall be made no later than 15 March. Time is of the essence." This meant the buyer could claim penalties even for a one-day delay.
A legal phrase used in contracts meaning that meeting the deadline is a fundamental requirement, not a guideline. If a contract contains this phrase, a supplier who delivers even one day late can be held in breach of contractWhen a party fails to fulfil a legal obligation under a contract. / Quando uma parte deixa de cumprir uma obrigação legal prevista em contrato..
Em português: Expressão jurídica usada em contratos que significa que o cumprimento do prazo é um requisito fundamental, não uma diretriz. Se um contrato contém essa frase, um fornecedor que entrega com apenas um dia de atraso pode ser considerado em descumprimento contratual.
Real example
"Supplier shall deliver the Goods to the Delivery Point no later than the date specified in the applicable Purchase Order. Time is of the essence."
Purchase Agreement, Clause 4.2
Contract Language
Penalty clause
Cláusula de Multa / Penalidade
Context clue
Procurement negotiation debrief
The contract included a Penalty clause of 0.5% per day of delay, capped at 10% of the total order value — this gave us real leverage during supplier negotiations.
A contract clause that specifies the financial compensation the supplier must pay if they fail to meet an obligation — most commonly late delivery. Usually expressed as a percentage of the order value per day of delay, with a maximum cap.
Em português: Cláusula contratual que especifica a compensação financeira que o fornecedor deve pagar se deixar de cumprir uma obrigação — mais comumente atraso na entrega. Geralmente expressa como porcentagem do valor do pedido por dia de atraso, com um limite máximo.
Real example
"In the event of late delivery, Supplier shall pay Buyer a penalty of 0.5% of the total Order value per calendar day of delay, up to a maximum of 10% of the Order value."
Purchase Agreement, Clause 7.1
Contract Language
Warranty
Garantia
Context clue
Contract review meeting
The supplier offered a 12-month Warranty, but we negotiated to start the period from installation rather than delivery — critical for equipment that sits in a warehouse for weeks.
A supplier's formal commitment that goods will be free from defects for a defined period. The warranty period, start date (from delivery, installation, or first use), and what it covers are all negotiable and must be clearly specified in the contract.
Em português: Compromisso formal do fornecedor de que os produtos estarão livres de defeitos por um período definido. O período de garantia, a data de início (a partir da entrega, instalação ou primeiro uso) e o que está coberto são negociáveis e devem estar claramente especificados no contrato.
Real example
"Supplier warrants that all Goods shall be free from defects in materials and workmanshipThe quality of how something is made — skill and care in production. / A qualidade de como algo é feito — habilidade e cuidado na produção. for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of delivery."
Purchase Agreement, Clause 9.3
Contract Language
Force majeure
Força Maior
Context clue
Dispute resolution email
The supplier invoked the Force majeure clause after a typhoon shut down their factory for three weeks, suspending their delivery obligation without penalty.
A contract clause that excuses a party from fulfilling its obligations when an extraordinary event beyond its control prevents performance — such as natural disasters, war, or pandemics. Buyers should always negotiate a time limit: if force majeureFrench for 'superior force' — events beyond anyone's control that prevent contract performance. / Eventos além do controle de qualquer pessoa que impedem o cumprimento contratual. continues beyond 60 days, either party may terminate.
Em português: Cláusula contratual que isenta uma parte de cumprir suas obrigações quando um evento extraordinário fora de seu controle impede a execução — como desastres naturais, guerra ou pandemias. Compradores devem sempre negociar um prazo limite: se a força maior continuar por mais de 60 dias, qualquer parte pode rescindir.
Real example
"Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations if such failure results from circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, war, government actions, or pandemic events."

Contract Language
In breach of contract
Em descumprimento contratual
Context clue
Procurement manager's email to legal
The supplier delivered two weeks late with no prior notice. Under the terms of the contract, they were immediately considered In breach of contract and the penalty clock started automatically.
When a party fails to fulfil one or more of its contractual obligations. A breach can be minor (partial non-performance) or material (serious failure that entitles the other party to terminate and claim damages). Late delivery, non-conforming goods, and failure to pay are common examples.
Em português: Quando uma parte deixa de cumprir uma ou mais de suas obrigações contratuais. O descumprimento pode ser leve (não execução parcial) ou material (falha grave que dá à outra parte o direito de rescindir e reclamar indenização). Entrega atrasada, produtos não conformes e falta de pagamento são exemplos comuns.
Real example
"Time is of the essence. A supplier who delivers late — even by one day — may be held in breach of contractWhen a party fails to fulfil a legal obligation under a contract. / Quando uma parte deixa de cumprir uma obrigação legal prevista em contrato. if this phrase appears in the delivery clause."

Amendment
Aditivo / Emenda
Context clue
Procurement team update
The delivery date in the original PO was 10 January, but due to port congestionWhen a port is too busy — ships wait longer to dock, causing delays. / Quando um porto está sobrecarregado — navios esperam mais para atracar, causando atrasos. we issued a formal Amendment extending it to 25 January and adjusting the penalty clause accordingly.
A formal document that modifies an existing contract or purchase order after it has been issued. Any change to the agreed price, delivery date, quantity, or terms must be processed through a formal amendment — informal agreements by email or phone are not enforceable.
Em português: Documento formal que modifica um contrato ou ordem de compra existente após sua emissão. Qualquer alteração no preço acordado, data de entrega, quantidade ou condições deve ser processada por meio de um aditivo formal — acordos informais por e-mail ou telefone não são juridicamente exigíveis.
Real example
"Any change to the price, delivery date, or quantity specified in this Purchase Order requires a written amendment signed by an authorised representative of both parties."

Quality
Non-conforming goods
Produtos Não Conformes
Context clue
Quality control inspection report
When the shipment arrived, the quality control team identified that 15% of the units were Non-conforming goods — the dimensions were outside the tolerance range specified in the PO.
Goods that do not meet the specifications, quality standards, or requirements defined in the contract or purchase order. The buyer has the right to reject non-conforming goods and request replacement or a price reduction.
Em português: Produtos que não atendem às especificações, padrões de qualidade ou requisitos definidos no contrato ou ordem de compra. O comprador tem o direito de rejeitar produtos não conformes e solicitar substituição ou redução de preço.
Real example
"Buyer shall inspect all Goods within ten (10) business days of delivery. Goods that do not conform to the specifications in the applicable Purchase Order may be rejected and returned at Supplier's cost."
Purchase Agreement, Clause 6.2
Finance
Net 30 / Net 60
Pagamento em 30 / 60 dias
Context clue
Finance and procurement alignment meeting
We proposed Net 30 / Net 60 as our standard payment condition, giving us two months to process the invoice internally and match it to the delivery receipt before releasing payment.
Standard payment terms meaning the buyer must pay within 30 (or 60) days of the invoice date. Net 60 is common in B2B procurement and gives the buyer more time to manage cash flow. Suppliers may offer early payment discounts (e.g. 2/10 Net 30 = 2% discount if paid within 10 days).
Em português: Condições de pagamento padrão que significam que o comprador deve pagar em até 30 (ou 60) dias após a data da fatura. Net 60 é comum em compras B2B e dá ao comprador mais tempo para gerenciar o fluxo de caixa. Fornecedores podem oferecer desconto por pagamento antecipado.
Real example
"Our standard payment terms are Net 60 from the date of receipt of a valid invoice. Please confirm whether these terms are acceptable before we proceed with the PO."
RFQ document, Section 4
Contract Language
Liability cap
Limite de Responsabilidade
Context clue
Legal and procurement review session
The supplier's draft agreement included a Liability cap equal to the value of the last invoice — which would cover only 1% of our potential losses in a product recallWhen a company retrieves products from customers due to a defect or safety issue. / Quando uma empresa recolhe produtos dos clientes por causa de defeito ou problema de segurança.When a company retrieves products from customers due to a safety defect. / Quando uma empresa recolhe produtos dos clientes por causa de um defeito de segurança. scenario.
A contractual limit on the maximum financial compensation one party can claim from the other. Suppliers often propose low liability caps to protect themselves from large claims. Buyers should always check if the cap covers the full value of the order — and negotiate upward if necessary.
Em português: Limite contratual sobre a compensação financeira máxima que uma parte pode reclamar da outra. Fornecedores frequentemente propõem caps baixos para se proteger de reclamações elevadas. Compradores devem sempre verificar se o cap cobre o valor total do pedido — e negociar para cima se necessário.
Real example
"Supplier's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total value of the Purchase Order(s) placed by Buyer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim."
Purchase Agreement, Clause 11.3
Contract Language
Arbitration
Arbitragem
Context clue
Purchase Agreement negotiation
Because both parties were in different countries, the contract specified Arbitration under ICC RulesRules set by the International Chamber of Commerce for conducting international arbitration. / Regras da Câmara de Comércio Internacional para arbitragem internacional. as the method for resolving any disputes — faster and more discreet than going to court.
A private dispute resolution process where a neutral third party (arbitrator) makes a binding decision instead of a court. Common in international contracts because it avoids the complexity of litigating across different legal systems. The ICC (International Chamber of Commerce) is a leading arbitration institution.
Em português: Processo privado de resolução de disputas em que um terceiro neutro (árbitro) toma uma decisão vinculante em vez de um tribunal. Comum em contratos internacionais porque evita a complexidade de litigar em diferentes sistemas jurídicos. A ICC (Câmara de Comércio Internacional) é uma instituição líder em arbitragem.

"Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC)."

Scope of supply
Escopo de Fornecimento
Context clue
Post-delivery dispute email
The supplier claimed that commissioningTesting and activating new equipment to confirm it works correctly before handover. / Testar e ativar novos equipamentos para confirmar que funcionam antes da entrega. and operator training were not part of the agreed Scope of supply, but our team found a clause in the Purchase Agreement that clearly included both.
The precise definition of what the supplier is responsible for delivering — including goods, services, documentation, packaging, and installation if applicable. A well-defined scope prevents disputes about what was and was not included in the contract price.
Em português: A definição precisa do que o fornecedor é responsável por entregar — incluindo produtos, serviços, documentação, embalagem e instalação, se aplicável. Um escopo bem definido evita disputas sobre o que estava ou não incluído no preço do contrato.

Purchase Order, Annex A
Contract Language
Confidentiality clause
Cláusula de Confidencialidade
Context clue
Pre-qualification process
Before sharing our product roadmap and cost structure with the potential supplier, we asked them to sign a Confidentiality clause to protect our intellectual property.
A contract clause that prohibits one or both parties from disclosing sensitive information — such as pricing, technical specifications, or business plans — to third parties. Also known as an NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) when it is a standalone document.
Em português: Cláusula contratual que proíbe uma ou ambas as partes de divulgar informações sensíveis — como preços, especificações técnicas ou planos de negócios — a terceiros. Também conhecida como NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) quando é um documento independente.
Real example
"Each party agrees to keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and not to disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent."
Purchase Agreement, Clause 15.1
