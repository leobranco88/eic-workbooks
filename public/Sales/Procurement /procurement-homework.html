C1
Sales · Proposal
Informal: "We need the client to give us written approval before we start." (obligation — contractual)
Model answer
"The client shall provide written approval before work commences."
Note: "Shall" makes the approval a contractual obligation on the client. "Need to" is informal and not suitable for a formal proposal or contract.
Nota: "Shall" torna a aprovação uma obrigação contratual do cliente. "Need to" é informal e inadequado para uma proposta ou contrato formal.
C2
Procurement · Supplier evaluation
Informal: "It would be a good idea if suppliers sent us their quality certificates every year." (recommendation — code of conduct)
Model answer
"Suppliers should submit updated quality certificates to Buyer on an annual basis."
Note: "Should" is the right modal for a recommended practice in a supplier code of conduct — expected, but not a hard legal obligation. "It would be a good idea if" is far too informal.
Nota: "Should" é o modal correto para uma prática recomendada em um código de conduta de fornecedores. "It would be a good idea if" é informal demais.
C3
Logistics · Import compliance
Informal: "All the product labels have to be in Portuguese or the shipment will be stopped at customs." (legal requirement)
Model answer
"All product labels must be in Portuguese, or the shipment will be held at customs."
Note: "Must" is correct for a legal compliance requirement. "Have to" is informal — and "must" in the formal version also sounds more authoritative in a shipping instruction or compliance note.
Nota: "Must" é correto para um requisito legal de conformidade. "Have to" é informal — e "must" na versão formal também soa mais autoritário.
C4
Sales · Contract termination
Informal: "If the supplier keeps missing deadlines, we can cancel the contract." (right/option — formal)
Model answer
"If the supplier fails to meet the agreed delivery dates, Buyer may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect."
Note: "May" makes termination a right — the buyer can exercise it but is not forced to. "Can" is informal and does not carry the same legal weight. "We can" is also too informal for a contract clause.
Nota: "May" torna a rescisão um direito — o comprador pode exercê-lo, mas não é obrigado. "Can" é informal e não tem o mesmo peso jurídico.
