Much Better, Not Very Better

Em português, "muito melhor" usa a mesma lógica de "muito bom". Em inglês, não — "very" nunca modifica um comparativo. "Very better" e "very more expensive" simplesmente não existem em inglês natural. Esta aula ensina o que usar no lugar, e quando usar cada intensificador.

Mariana Voss · comparando VanderChem vs. Halvorsen
01The "Very" Trap

Antes do vocabulário, o erro que gera tudo isso.

Wrong vs. Right
✗ "VanderChem's export license is very more complicated than Halvorsen's."
✓ "VanderChem's export license is much more complicated than Halvorsen's."

✗ "Halvorsen's onboarding process was very faster."
✓ "Halvorsen's onboarding process was way faster." (informal) / "much faster" (neutral)
The rule: "very" only goes with the plain adjective (very good, very expensive, very fast). The moment the adjective becomes a comparative — "-er" or "more ___" — you need a different word. That's what this lesson is about.
02Big Difference

Quando a diferença é grande — passe o mouse pra ver a tradução.

much + comparative — the neutral, all-purpose choice: "much better," "much cheaper"
a lot + comparative — slightly more casual than "much": "a lot faster"
far + comparative — a bit more formal or emphatic: "far more reliable"
way + comparative — strong and informal, common in speech: "way better"
even + comparative — adds surprise: the gap is bigger than you'd expect
In Mariana's words
"Halvorsen's quote came in much lower than VanderChem's — not just a little lower, a lot lower. And their onboarding process was far more transparent. Honestly, their communication has been even better than I expected."
03Small Difference

Quando a diferença é pequena, os intensificadores mudam completamente.

a bit + comparative — casual, common in speech: "a bit higher"
a little + comparative — very close in meaning to "a bit": "a little more expensive"
slightly + comparative — more formal/written than "a bit": "slightly higher"
marginally + comparative — the smallest possible difference, often in reports/data
In Mariana's words
"To be fair, Halvorsen's lead time is only slightly shorter than VanderChem's — maybe two days. And their certification score was just marginally higher. It's not a huge gap on paper, but the reliability difference feels a lot bigger in practice."
04The Comparison Report

A fala que a Mariana levaria pra reunião com a diretoria — a mesma reunião mencionada no Story Search.

Team Meeting · Mariana presents the supplier comparison
MarianaOn price, Halvorsen is much cheaper — about 15% lower per batch.
DavidAnd on delivery time?
MarianaOnly slightly faster, honestly. A couple of days at most. That's not the deciding factor.
DavidSo what is?
MarianaTrust. Halvorsen's communication has been far more transparent than VanderChem's ever was. And after the export license issue, that matters even more than price.
