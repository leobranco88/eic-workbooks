Incoterms — who pays, and when the risk transfers

"Who pays" não é só sobre repassar custo — no comércio internacional, existe um conjunto de regras padronizadas pra isso: os Incoterms. Eles decidem quem paga o frete, o seguro, a alfândega, e — mais importante — em que ponto exato o risco passa do vendedor pro comprador.

01The Big Four

Do mínimo esforço do vendedor até o máximo — quatro Incoterms que cobrem a maioria das situações reais.

Seller does less
Seller does more
EXW
Ex Works
Transportbuyer arranges & pays for everything
Insurancebuyer's responsibility
Customsbuyer handles both export & import
Risk transfers the moment the goods are made available at the seller's door.
FOB
Free On Board
Transportseller pays to the port; buyer pays the rest
Insurancebuyer's responsibility
Customsseller handles export; buyer handles import
Risk transfers once the goods are loaded on board the vessel.
CIF
Cost, Insurance & Freight
Transportseller pays all the way to the destination port
Insuranceseller pays for it
Customsseller handles export; buyer handles import
Risk still transfers at loading — even though the seller keeps paying after that.
DDP
Delivered Duty Paid
Transportseller arranges & pays for everything
Insuranceseller's responsibility
Customsseller handles both export & import, duties included
Risk transfers only when goods arrive at the buyer's door, cleared and ready.
02The Classic Trap

O ponto que mais confunde estudante e profissional junto: em CIF, o vendedor paga o frete e o seguro até o porto de destino — mas o risco já passou pro comprador desde o embarque. Pagar não é o mesmo que ser dono do risco.

In context — Ana Claudia, Althea Industrial Imports
"The supplier in Stuttgart kept insisting they'd already paid for insurance, since the shipment was under CIF terms. But that's not the point — risk transferred to Althea the moment the goods were loaded onto the vessel. Who pays for insurance and who is liable for the damage are two different questions."
03Talking About Incoterms

O vocabulário que você usa pra explicar quem é responsável por quê — passe o mouse nas expressões pra ver a tradução.

risk transfers — the moment responsibility for loss or damage moves from seller to buyer
responsible for — who has to arrange or pay for something
liable for loss or damage — who has to cover the cost if something goes wrong in transit
the seller's/buyer's obligation ends at — the exact point where one side's duty stops
under [Incoterm] terms — e.g. "under FOB terms," "under DDP terms"

liable — as in "liable for loss or damage."

obligation — as in "the seller's obligation ends at."
09
If the goods are damaged in transit under DDP terms, it's the supplier who remains ______ for the loss.
liable — under DDP, the seller keeps the risk almost the whole way.
