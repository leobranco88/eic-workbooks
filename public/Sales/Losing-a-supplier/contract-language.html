Contract Language — citing clauses & assigning cost

Duas coisas que aparecem toda vez que um fornecedor dá problema: alguém precisa citar o que está no contrato, e alguém precisa decidir quem paga a conta. Este é o vocabulário pra fazer as duas coisas com confiança.

01Citing the Contract

Como dizer que algo "está escrito", "está declarado" ou "de acordo com" o contrato — passe o mouse nas expressões pra ver a tradução.

written in the contract — the most literal way to say it
stated in the contract — slightly more formal
specified in the contract — for exact numbers, dates, or quantities
according to the contract — the most common way to introduce a clause
the contract states that... — use when quoting the content directly
breach of contract — when one side fails to meet its obligations
In context
"According to the contract, VanderChem must give 30 days' notice before any price change. Missing three delivery dates in a row without notice could even be considered a breach of contract."
02Who Pays

Quando o custo sobe, alguém tem que decidir o que fazer com ele: repassar, absorver ou dividir.

pass the cost on to the customer — the most common phrase for this
absorb the cost — to take the loss yourself instead
shift the cost to — slightly more informal than "pass on"
transfer the cost to — common in contracts and finance
compliance-adjusted (delivery date) — a new date that accounts for a legal requirement
hold someone to the contract — to insist that the other side meet its terms
In context
"BioSource decided to absorb part of the increase instead of passing all of it on to the customer — but Frigorex made it clear they would hold BioSource to the original terms."

specified — used for exact numbers and details.
05

states — used when quoting the content directly.
06
Missing three delivery dates in a row could be considered a ______ of contract.
breach — a failure to meet the contract's terms.
07

shift — to move part of the cost onto someone else.
08
BioSource chose to ______ the increase rather than raise its own prices.
absorb — to take the cost yourself instead of passing it on.
09
In the end, unable to absorb any more losses, Mariana had to ______ the extra cost on to the customer.
pass — as in "pass the cost on to."
10
Some contracts include a clause that lets a supplier ______ part of the cost to a third party.
transfer — the more formal option, common in contracts.
11
Given the new export regulation, VanderChem should offer a ______ delivery date.
compliance-adjusted — a date that accounts for a new legal requirement.
12
Frigorex made it clear they would ______ BioSource to the terms of the contract, no exceptions.
hold — as in "hold someone to the contract."
