Ache o erro e corrija a frase. Algumas já estão certas.

Rewrite the sentence with the mistake fixed. If it is already right, press the button.

Nem toda frase tem erro. Os erros possíveis são: “of” a mais, “for” antes de verbo, “but” junto com “although”, sujeito e verbo depois de “because of”, ou falta de “could”.

Example · exemplo resolvido

We waited during two hoursfor two hours.

We waited for two hours.

“Two hours” diz quanto tempo durou: “for”, não “during”.

Join it

Junte as duas frases com o marker pedido. Se depois do marker vem um nome, encolha a ideia.

Join the two sentences with the marker.

Use o marker que está em negrito. Com “for … to”, ponha uma pessoa entre as duas palavras. Quando o marker abre a frase, ponha uma vírgula depois da primeira ideia.

Example · exemplo resolvido

It was cold. We went swimming.

Join them with although.

Although it was cold, we went swimming.

“Although” traz sujeito e verbo (it was cold). Com o marker no início, a vírgula separa as duas ideias.

Markers · Mix it
Which one?
because · so · although · whilesujeito + verbo

Although the film was long, I enjoyed it.

Each side has its own subject and verb. Never although + but.

because of · due to · thanks tosó um nome

The match stopped because of the storm.

A noun after it. Thanks to is a good reason.

despite · in spite ofum nome ou -ing

Despite the noise, he fell asleep.

Despite has no “of”. In spite of has.

during · for · whilequando e quanto tempo

He called during dinner. We waited for an hour.

During + noun. For + how long. While + subject and verb.

to · so thatpara quê

I called to ask. I wrote it down so that he could read it.

Same subject: to + verb. Other subject: so that + could.

for her tooutra pessoa

I kept a seat for my aunt to sit in.

Person after for: me, you, him, her, us, them. Then to + verb.
