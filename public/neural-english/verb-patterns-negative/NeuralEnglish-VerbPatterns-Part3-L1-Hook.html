Quick reference · consulta rápida
The three families
1Family 1 · most verbs
Verb + object + to
want, need, ask, tell, advise, expect, allow, encourage, persuade, remind, warn, invite, convince, teach, force, would like
New: get, urge, beg, permit, require, enable, inspire, trust, dare, would love, would hate, would prefer · help (with or without to)
I want you to call her.Eu quero que você ligue pra ela.
not goes before to: I told him not to eat it.
2Family 2 · three verbs, no to
let · make · have + object + verb
let, make, have · have = aquilo é função da pessoa
New: see, hear, watch, feel, notice · help
Let me carry that bag.Deixa eu carregar essa sacola.
3Family 3 · keeps that · formal
suggest · recommend + that + subject + verb
suggest, recommend, insist, demand, propose, request
New: it’s important / essential / vital that · advise, ask, urge, require (also Family 1)
The teacher suggested that we read more.A professora sugeriu que a gente lesse mais.
Same bare verb for everyone, even he, she, it: that he go, that she be. Never goes, never is.

Quick reference · consulta rápida
Question families
AFamily A · flip it · inverte
Já tem auxiliar na frase: be, can, will, would, should…
auxiliary + person + verb
Will your parents let you go?Seus pais vão te deixar ir?
Short answer: Yes, they will. Nunca entra “do”: Can you…?, não Do you can…?
BFamily B · add it · adiciona
Não tem auxiliar: entra do, does ou did.
do · does · did + person + base verb
Did she tell you to wait?Ela te mandou esperar?
O passado vai para o “did”: Did she tell, nunca Did she told. Short answer: Yes, she did.
CFamily C · wh first · palavra na frente
Você não sabe a ação, a coisa, o lugar: what, who, where na frente.
what · who · where + A or B
What do you want me to do?O que você quer que eu faça?
Depois do “what”, a pergunta é A ou B normal. Perguntando a ação, o “do” fica no fim.
DFamily D · keep it · não mexeLesson 2
O who ou o what é o sujeito: quem fez, o que causou.
who · what + verb
Who told you to come?Quem te mandou vir?
Nada pula, nada entra: Who told, nunca Who did tell.
EFamily E · hide it · escondeLesson 3
A pergunta fica dentro de outra frase: Do you know…? Can you tell me…?
Do you know + what + person + verb
Do you know what she wants me to do?Você sabe o que ela quer que eu faça?
Dentro da frase, ordem de afirmação: what she wants, nunca what does she want.

Toda pergunta tem duas famílias: a letra diz como montar a pergunta, o número é o verb pattern.
