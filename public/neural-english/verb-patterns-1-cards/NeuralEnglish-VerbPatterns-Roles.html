Speaking · Verb Patterns · Part 1

Who are
you? Guess.

One player draws a role card. The others ask “Do you…?” questions and work out who the person is.

Um aluno sorteia um cartão de papel (parent, boss, doctor…). Os outros perguntam e descobrem quem é, usando o banco de chunks. No fim, contam para a turma: she makes, he asks. O ponto da atividade é o padrão do verbo e o -s no relato.

1 Before you start

Ask, answer, report

Pergunte, responda, conte

Three voices: you ask, I answer, and later she or he tells the class.

O padrão do verbo não muda do começo ao fim: make them do, ask them to help, suggest that he read. O que muda no relato é o -s do verbo principal.

The game · o jogo
ADo you tell them to rest?
MartaYes, I tell them to rest.
ADo you make them do homework?
MartaNo, I don’t make them do homework.
ADo you let them go back to work?
MartaYes, I let them go back to work.
AAre you a doctor?
MartaYes, I am!
The report · para a turma

Marta is a doctor. She tells them to rest. She lets them go back to work. She doesn’t make them do homework.

3 Play

Draw, ask, guess

Sorteie, pergunte, descubra
  1. 1
    Draw a role card. Only you see it. Your six chunks are your “yes”.
    Sorteie um cartão de papel. Só quem tirou vê o cartão: os 6 chunks dele são os “yes”.
  2. 2
    Ask your partner from the whole bank: Do you…?
    O parceiro pergunta, escolhendo no banco inteiro: Do you…?
  3. 3
    Answer in a full sentence: “Yes, I make them…” or “No, I don’t let them…”.
    Resposta em frase completa. Yes para os chunks do cartão, no para o resto.
  4. 4
    Guess after six questions: “Are you a…?”. Three guesses.
    Depois de 6 perguntas, o parceiro chuta: “Are you a…?”. Três chutes.
  5. 5
    Tell the class: “She is a doctor. She tells them to rest.”
    O parceiro conta para a turma: “She is a doctor. She tells them to rest.”

4 Tell the class

She makes, he lets

Na hora de contar, o verbo principal ganha -s

Start with My partner is a… Then say three things about the people.

Só o verbo principal ganha o -s. O que vem depois fica igual: she makes them do, he asks them to help. Para o não, use doesn’t, e o verbo volta sem -s: she doesn’t let them go.

+ sask → asks · tell → tells · let → lets · make → makes · want → wants
+ sadvise → advises · encourage → encourages · suggest → suggests
havehave → has: she has them read aloud
after thatno -s: she suggests that he read
The usual mistakes
Os erros de sempre, com a caneta do professor
She makesmake her kids do homework.
Ela obriga os filhos a fazer a lição. No relato, o verbo ganha -s.
He lets them stayto stay up late.
Ele deixa eles ficarem acordados até tarde. Com let, sem to.
She asks them to helphelp at home.
Ela pede para eles ajudarem em casa. Com ask, o to volta.
The doctor suggests that she walkwalks every day.
O médico sugere que ela caminhe todo dia. Depois do that, nada de -s.
He tells them not todon’t be late.
Ele manda eles não se atrasarem. O não vem antes do to: not to.
