Entrevista individual, 5–8 minutos. Comece pelo aquecimento, depois siga pelas faixas até o aluno começar a ter dificuldade real — não precisa fazer todas as perguntas de todas as faixas. Use a rubrica no final para pontuar.
AQUECIMENTO

Warm-up (todos os níveis)

  • What's your name? How old are you?
  • Do you have any brothers or sisters?
  • What do you usually do on the weekend?
ROTEIRO

Perguntas por Faixa

Comece na Faixa 1. Suba de faixa enquanto o aluno responder com fluência razoável.

Life 1-2Present Simple, personal info
  • Where are you from?
  • What's your favorite color?
  • Do you like sports? Which ones?
Life 2-3Comparatives, routines, abilities
  • Tell me about your daily routine. What time do you wake up?
  • Is your city bigger or smaller than the capital?
  • What can you do really well?
Life 3-4Present Perfect, Past Simple
  • What did you do last weekend?
  • Have you ever traveled to another country? Tell me about it.
  • How long have you studied English?
Life 4-5Conditionals, opinions
  • If you could visit any country, where would you go? Why?
  • What do you think about social media? Is it good or bad?
  • What would you do if you found a wallet on the street?
Life 5-6Speculation, hypotheticals, abstract topics
  • What do you think your life will be like in 10 years?
  • Do you agree that technology makes people less social? Why or why not?
  • If you could change one thing about your school, what would it be?
TAREFA

Picture Description

Escolha UMA das duas cenas abaixo (ou use as duas em aplicações diferentes). Peça: 'Can you describe what you see? What are the people doing?' Dê cerca de 1 minuto de fala com o mínimo de ajuda.

Opção A — No parque
  • What can you see in the picture? What's the weather like?
  • What are the people in the park doing? Can you find three different activities?
  • What's the difference between the people having a picnic and the people doing yoga?
  • Why do you think so many people are in the park today? What do you think will happen if it rains?
Opção B — Em casa
  • What room is this? How many people can you see?
  • What is each person doing right now?
  • What's the difference between what the teenager on the sofa is doing and what the boy near the sink is doing?
  • Do you think this family spends a lot of time together? Why do you think that?

Marque de 0 a 4 em cada critério (0 = nível mais baixo da linha, 4 = nível mais alto).
