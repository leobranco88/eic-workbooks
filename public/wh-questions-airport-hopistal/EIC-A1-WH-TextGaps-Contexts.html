A Day at the Hospital

Nina always goes to the doctor for a check-up 1(time). It's a habit she started three years ago. She says it helps her feel in control of her health.

Kai came to the hospital 2(reason). He was playing football when he fell and hurt his ankle. The pain started slowly but got much worse during the night.

Kai waited for about thirty minutes and then a doctor came in. She examined his ankle carefully. He was treated by 3(person) — she was calm and very professional.

Dr. Sofia is very busy this morning. She is 4(thing) right now. There are five patients waiting and she wants to see all of them before lunch.

When Nina arrived, the waiting room was almost full. She found a seat 5(place) and waited quietly. She read a magazine while she waited for her name to be called.

Dr. Sofia asked Kai to describe the pain. He described it 6(manner). She listened carefully and decided to order an X-ray.

The X-ray result came back quickly. It showed 7(thing). Dr. Sofia explained the result clearly and Kai felt relieved.

Dr. Sofia is now talking to Kai about the next steps. She is explaining the treatment 8(manner). She always makes sure her patients understand everything.

Nina's appointment was at 10am, but the clinic was running late. Her appointment actually started 9(time). She didn't mind — she was used to waiting at this clinic.

After the appointment, Kai felt tired. He would prefer to rest 10(place). He doesn't like staying in hospitals longer than necessary.

Dr. Sofia always gives her patients 11(thing) at the end of each appointment. She thinks written instructions are more helpful than verbal ones. Most patients forget what they hear but remember what they read.

Kai is feeling better now. He is going to 12(place) after he leaves the hospital. His friend is already waiting outside to pick him up.

A Busy Day at the Airport

Rafael travels for work very often. He usually arrives at the airport 1(time) before his flight. He likes to have time to check in, have a coffee, and relax.

This morning, Rafael went straight to the check-in area. He checked in 2(place) because he had no bags to check. The process took less than two minutes.

Camila arrived at the airport later than she planned. She was running 3(reason). Her first flight landed late and she had only forty minutes to reach her next gate.

Camila is looking for help at the airport. She is talking to 4(person) right now. She needs to know the fastest way to Gate 22.

André waited at the baggage carousel for twenty minutes. He discovered 5(thing) when the carousel stopped. His suitcase was not there — it never arrived.

André went to the lost baggage desk to report the problem. He felt 6(manner) but stayed calm and polite. The agent apologised and started filling out a report.

After going through security, Rafael had some time before boarding. He waited 7(place) and ordered a black coffee. He read the news on his phone while he waited.

The airline agent is speaking to André about the situation. She is offering him 8(thing) while they look for his luggage. André is writing down all the details carefully.

Camila ran through the terminal as fast as she could. She reached Gate 22 9(time). The gate was still open, but boarding had already started.

Rafael always boards early. He boards 10(manner) because he prefers a window seat and extra space. He never waits for the last call.

Camila is on the plane now, but she is still nervous. She is nervous 11(reason). She hopes the connection went through and her seat is confirmed.

André is not happy with the situation. He would prefer 12(thing). He has an important meeting tomorrow and needs his clothes.
