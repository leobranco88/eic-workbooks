01My Two Favourite Neighbourhoods
Leitura · By Yuki Tanaka
Shimokitazawa and Ginza, Tokyo

Shimokitazawa and Ginza are both in Tokyo, but they feel like different planets. Ginza is way more polished: glass buildings, luxury brands, everything perfectly in place. Shimokitazawa is far more chaotic, in the best way: narrow alleys, secondhand shops, live music spilling out of tiny bars.

Prices are not as far apart as you'd expect. A coffee in Shimokitazawa is only slightly cheaper than one in Ginza, maybe fifty yen difference. The real gap is in what you buy, not what you pay.

Ginza is nowhere near as walkable at night as Shimokitazawa. After 9pm, Ginza empties out, quiet and closed. Shimokitazawa gets louder, busier, more alive.

Still, both neighbourhoods are just as essential to understanding Tokyo. One shows you the city's polish. The other shows you its texture. You need both to see the whole picture.

02Medindo a diferença
Quick reference
Diferença enorme
way / far / a lot / much

A diferença é grande e óbvia.

"way more expensive"

Diferença pequena
slightly / a bit / a little

A diferença é pequena, quase imperceptível.

"slightly faster"

Sem diferença
just as ... as

As duas coisas são iguais nesse aspecto.

"just as good as"

Sem vantagem
not / nowhere near as ... as

Uma coisa fica claramente abaixo da outra.

"nowhere near as good as"

Lembrete: "way" e "far" só combinam com comparativos ("way better"), nunca com "as...as". Nunca "way as good as".
ContextoEstruturaExemplo
Mochilas de 1.2kg vs. 4.8kgDiferença enormeThis one is way lighter than that one.

Dois festivais com o mesmo públicoSem diferençaThis festival is just as popular as that one.
