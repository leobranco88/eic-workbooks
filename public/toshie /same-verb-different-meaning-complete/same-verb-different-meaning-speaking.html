Same Verb
— Now You Ask

No more choosing the right answer — now you create the question. Combines all 12 verbs from both modules (look, think, dream, care, agree, talk, wait, get, turn, put, take, run) in a fully spoken practice.
頑張れ
How today works

Both activities require you to produce the sentence, not choose between options. In the first, you ask and the teacher answers for real — genuine conversation, not mechanical drilling. In the second, you read a situation and create the question on the spot; the "example" that shows up after is just one possible idea, not the only right answer.

01 Ask Your Teacher 16 questions

Ask the question out loud, using the verb + preposition shown. The teacher answers for real. There's no wrong answer here — just a well-formed or poorly-formed question.

1
get over
Ask about something (an illness, a disappointment) your teacher has gotten over.
先生が乗り越えたことについて質問してください。
Start with one of these
What's something you've gotten over?Was it hard to get over...?How long did it take you to get over it?
2
get on
Ask if your teacher gets on well with all of their coworkers.
先生が同僚全員とうまくやっているか質問してください。
Start with one of these
Do you get on well with...?Have you always gotten on with...?What makes you get on so well with...?
3
take up
Ask about a hobby your teacher has taken up recently.
先生が最近始めた趣味について質問してください。
Start with one of these
What have you taken up recently?Why did you decide to take up...?When did you take up...?
4
agree to
Ask about something your teacher would never agree to do at work.
先生が仕事で絶対に受け入れないことについて質問してください。
Start with one of these
Is there something you'd never agree to?Would you ever agree to...?What's the last thing you refused to agree to?
5
put up with
Ask about something your teacher can't put up with (noise, behavior).
先生が我慢できないことについて質問してください。
Start with one of these
What can't you put up with?Is there something you just can't put up with at work?How do you put up with...?
6
dream of
Ask if your teacher dreams of living in another country someday.
先生がいつか別の国に住むことを夢見ているか質問してください。
Start with one of these
Do you dream of living abroad?Have you ever dreamed of...?What country do you dream of living in?
7
run into
Ask about the last time your teacher ran into someone by chance on the street.
先生が最後に道で誰かに偶然会ったのはいつか質問してください。
Start with one of these
When was the last time you ran into someone?Have you ever run into an old friend by chance?Who did you run into recently?
8
look after
Ask who looks after your teacher's home or pets when they travel.
先生が旅行するとき、誰が家やペットの面倒を見るか質問してください。
Start with one of these
Who looks after your place when you travel?Does someone look after your pets while you're away?Who do you ask to look after things when you're gone?
9
turn down
Ask about something your teacher turned down recently (an invitation, an offer).
先生が最近断ったこと（誘いや申し出）について質問してください。
Start with one of these
Have you turned anything down recently?What's the last offer you turned down?Why did you decide to turn it down?
10
take after
Ask if your teacher takes after their mother or their father more.
先生が母親と父親のどちらに似ているか質問してください。
Start with one of these
Who do you take after more, your mom or your dad?Do people say you take after your father?In what ways do you take after your mother?
11
think about
Ask about something your teacher is thinking about changing in their life.
先生が人生で変えようと考えていることについて質問してください。
Start with one of these
Is there anything you're thinking about changing?What have you been thinking about lately?Are you thinking about doing anything differently?
12
run out of
Ask if your teacher has ever run out of something important on a trip (money, battery).
先生が旅行中に大事な物（お金やバッテリー）を切らしたことがあるか質問してください。
Start with one of these
Have you ever run out of money on a trip?What's something you ran out of while traveling?Did you ever run out of battery at the worst time?
13
turn to
Ask who your teacher turns to when they have a problem.
先生が問題を抱えたとき、誰に頼るか質問してください。
Start with one of these
Who do you turn to when you have a problem?What do you turn to when you're stressed?Is there someone you always turn to for advice?
14
look forward to
Ask about something your teacher is really looking forward to this year.
先生が今年とても楽しみにしていることについて質問してください。
Start with one of these
What are you looking forward to this year?Is there something you're really looking forward to?What's the thing you look forward to the most?
15
talk (sb) into
Ask if anyone has ever talked your teacher into doing something they didn't want to do.
誰かが先生を説得して、やりたくなかったことをさせたことがあるか質問してください。
Start with one of these
Has anyone ever talked you into doing something you didn't want to?What's something someone talked you into?Who's good at talking you into things?
16
care about
Ask about something your teacher really cares about at work.
先生が仕事で本当に大切にしていることについて質問してください。
Start with one of these
What do you care about most at work?Is there something you really care about in your job?What do you care about more than anything else at work?
02 Scenario → Your Question 12 scenarios

Read the situation, imagine you're talking to this person, and create the question on the spot — out loud or in writing. The example that shows up after is just one idea, not the only correct form.

1.A friend of yours ended a long relationship a few months ago.友達が長い関係を数ヶ月前に終えた。
get over
Start with one of these
Have you gotten over it yet?Is it hard to get over something like this?How are you getting over it?
Possible example"Have you gotten over the breakup yet?"
2.Your coworker showed up to the meeting without warning, an hour late.同僚が連絡もなく、会議に一時間遅れて来た。
turn up
Start with one of these
Why did you turn up so late?What happened, you turned up an hour late!Is everything okay? You turned up without any warning.
Possible example"Why did you turn up so late?"
3.Someone at work is always criticizing coworkers in front of everyone.職場のある人が、いつもみんなの前で同僚を批判している。
put down
Start with one of these
Why do you always put people down like that?Don't you think it's unfair to put them down in front of everyone?How would you feel if someone put you down like that?
Possible example"Why do you always put people down like that?"
4.A friend of yours recently inherited and took over the family business.友達が最近、家族の会社を引き継いだ。
take over
Start with one of these
How does it feel to take over the family business?What was it like to take over from your parents?Were you ready to take it over?
Possible example"How does it feel to take over the family business?"
5.You heard that an acquaintance is going to run for city council.知り合いが市議会議員に立候補すると聞いた。
run for
Start with one of these
Why did you decide to run for city council?What made you want to run for office?Are you nervous about running for mayor?
Possible example"Why did you decide to run for city council?"
6.A coworker is always trying a new hobby, different from the last one.同僚がいつも前と違う新しい趣味に挑戦している。
get into
Start with one of these
What made you get into pottery?How did you get into painting?What do you like about getting into new hobbies?
Possible example"What made you get into pottery?"
7.Your neighbor keeps complaining about the street noise, but never moves.隣人はいつも道路の騒音について文句を言っているが、引っ越さない。
put up with
Start with one of these
How do you put up with all this noise?Why don't you just move if you can't put up with it?Doesn't it bother you to put up with this every day?
Possible example"How do you put up with all this noise?"
8.A friend turned down a job offer that seemed amazing.友達が素晴らしく見える仕事のオファーを断った。
turn down
Start with one of these
Why did you turn down such a great offer?Weren't you tempted to accept instead of turning it down?Do you regret turning it down?
Possible example"Why did you turn down such a great offer?"
9.An acquaintance of yours treats people who earn less than him very badly.知り合いが自分より稼ぎの少ない人をひどく扱う。
look down on
Start with one of these
Why do you look down on people like that?Don't you think it's wrong to look down on someone for that?How can you look down on people who work just as hard as you?
Possible example"Why do you look down on people like that?"
10.Your friend is learning to play a new instrument.友達が新しい楽器を習っている。
take up
Start with one of these
When did you take up the violin?What made you want to take up a new instrument?Is it hard to take up something new at this stage?
Possible example"When did you take up the violin?"
11.A coworker always arrives late and is never reprimanded for it.ある同僚はいつも遅刻するのに、決して叱られない。
get away with
Start with one of these
How do you always get away with being late?Doesn't anyone notice that you get away with it every time?What's your secret for getting away with that?
Possible example"How do you always get away with being late?"
12.A friend of yours always turns to reading when she's stressed.友達はストレスを感じるといつも読書に頼る。
turn to
Start with one of these
Why do you always turn to books when you're stressed?What is it about books that makes you turn to them?Do you ever turn to anything else when you're overwhelmed?
Possible example"Why do you always turn to books when you're stressed?"
