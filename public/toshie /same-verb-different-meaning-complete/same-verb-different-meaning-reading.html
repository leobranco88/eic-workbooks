01 Reading ~260 words
The verb combinations from Part 1 and Part 2 are bolded in the text. Before you read the questions, try covering the story and recalling which ones you remember.
A Regular Monday

Sena's alarm goes off at 6:30, and she's already thinking about the presentation she has today. She's been putting off finishing the slides for two days, and now there's no time left to wait for inspiration.

At the office, she doesn't want to wait for the elevator, so she takes the stairs instead. On her way up, she runs into Rio, who looks exhausted. He's been trying to get over a cold all week.

"Are you getting by okay?" she asks.

"Slowly," he says. "I'm just trying to get through today without falling asleep in the meeting. I looked into a few home remedies last night, but nothing's working yet."

They talk about their weekend plans while waiting for the coffee machine to finish. Sena mentions she dreams of a quiet Saturday — no meetings, no emails, just coffee and a good book.

During the meeting, Sena's boss, Marcos, doesn't agree with the new deadline, but the client won't turn down a discount, so the team has to figure something out. Sena cares about doing the project well — she took it on herself, even though nobody else wanted to.

Marcos turns to her and asks her to run the numbers by the finance team before lunch. She agrees to send the report by noon, even though she's not sure she'll get through the whole spreadsheet in time.

At lunch, she puts on her jacket and takes a short walk outside. She looks forward to the weekend more than ever. For now, though, she has to get back to her desk and put up with one more afternoon of spreadsheets.

02 Comprehension Questions 10 items

Answer in your own words, in full sentences. These aren't yes/no questions — explain what's happening and why.

1.What is Sena thinking about when her alarm goes off, and why hasn't she finished it yet?
Sample answerShe's thinking about the presentation she has today. She hasn't finished it because she's been putting off finishing the slides for two days.
2.Why does Rio look exhausted at the office, and what has he tried to do about it?
Sample answerHe looks exhausted because he's been trying to get over a cold all week. He looked into a few home remedies, but nothing has worked yet.
3.What does Sena say she dreams of doing this weekend? Describe it in your own words.
Sample answerShe dreams of having a quiet Saturday with no meetings and no emails — just coffee and a good book.
4.Why doesn't Marcos agree with the new deadline?
Sample answerThe text doesn't give an exact reason, but the new deadline seems tighter because the client won't turn down a discount, which makes things harder for the team.
5.What does the client refuse to do, and how does that affect the team?
Sample answerThe client won't turn down (give up) a discount, so the team has to find a way to make the project work within a tighter budget or timeline.
6.Why did Sena decide to take the project on herself?
Sample answerShe took the project on herself because she cares about doing it well, even though nobody else on the team wanted to take responsibility for it.
7.What does Marcos ask Sena to do during the meeting, and what does she agree to?
Sample answerMarcos asks her to run the numbers by the finance team before lunch. She agrees to send the report by noon.
8.Describe what Sena does during her lunch break, step by step.
Sample answerShe puts on her jacket, takes a short walk outside, and thinks about how much she's looking forward to the weekend.
9.In your own words, why does Sena look forward to the weekend so much?
Sample answerShe's had a stressful, busy day full of deadlines and meetings, so she's looking forward to a calm weekend with no work responsibilities.
10.How does Sena's afternoon end, according to the last line of the text?
Sample answerShe has to go back to her desk and put up with one more afternoon of working on spreadsheets.
