02 Adjective + Preposition · Story Gap-Fill 12 items

Read each short scene and complete the gaps with the correct preposition.

Quick reference
INinterested
OFafraid, proud, aware
ABOUTworried
FORfamous, responsible
TOsimilar
FROMdifferent
WITHsatisfied
ATsurprised, good
A · The Old Photographer
He'd always been interested in black-and-white photography, ever since his father gave him an old camera. He wasn't afraid of criticism — every wall of his small studio was covered with unsold prints. His daughter was proud of him, even though she'd never say it out loud. And though the shop was small, he seemed perfectly satisfied with the life he'd built there.
B · The New Neighbours
The new neighbours were nothing like the old ones — completely different from what we expected. At first we were surprised at how quiet they were. Within a month, though, we discovered they were famous for their weekend barbecues, and the whole street was suddenly aware of exactly when summer had arrived.
C · The Mountain Guide
As a mountain guide, she was responsible for every hiker on the trail, rain or shine. She was good at reading the weather long before the apps could. Some tourists were worried about the sudden fog, but she stayed calm — her methods were similar to the ones her own grandfather had used decades earlier.
03 Questions Ending in a Preposition · Rewrite 10 items

Turn each statement into a question ending in a preposition. Remember the engine: find the preposition partner → turn the object into what/who → strand the preposition at the end.

1.
He is worried about the flight delay. → What is he worried about?
2.
She relies on her neighbour for groceries. → Who does she rely on?
3.
The dish is similar to something from her childhood. → What is the dish similar to?
4.
They are grateful to the local guide. → Who are they grateful to?
5.
He applied for a research grant. → What did he apply for?
6.
She is responsible for the entire garden. → What is she responsible for?
7.
The museum is dealing with a funding problem. → What is the museum dealing with?
8.
He's proud of his youngest daughter. → Who is he proud of?
9.
The plan is different from what we agreed. → What is the plan different from?
10.
She's good at negotiating prices. → What is she good at?
