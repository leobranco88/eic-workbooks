01Two Verbs, Two Flavorsreference

Both mean "someone else does it for you" — but have is a neutral request, and get implies some effort or persuasion. Each also has an "active" form (naming who does it) and a "service" form (the agent is dropped).

HAVE · active
have + person + base verb
arrange — expected, no persuasion
I had the plumber fix the leak.
HAVE · service
have + thing + past participle
arranged for you — agent unnamed
I had the leak fixed.
GET · active
get + person + to + verb
persuade — some effort involved
I got the plumber to fix the leak on a Sunday.
GET · service
get + thing + past participle
same idea, more effort/achievement flavor
I finally got the leak fixed.
