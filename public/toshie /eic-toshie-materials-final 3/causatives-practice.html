01Referencefive verbs

Same idea — someone causes someone else to act — five different meanings.

VerbStructureMeaningExample
MAKEmake + object + base verb / adjectiveforce — no choiceSchools make first-graders carry their own trays.
LETlet + object + base verbpermission — full choiceParents don't let a six-year-old ride alone.
GETget + object + to + verbpersuade — takes effortIt gets visiting foreigners to ask questions.
HAVEhave + object + base verbarrange — it's their roleA mother might have her own mother pick the kids up.
HELPhelp + object + (to) + verbassist — togetherA parent might help a child pack the bag.
02Fill in the Gaps15 items

Choose the correct causative verb, in the correct tense, for each blank.

worked example
My dad ____ me finish my dinner before I could leave the table.
No choice, an order — that's make.
→ My dad made me finish my dinner.
01
My dad        me finish my dinner before I could leave the table.
AnswermadeNo choice — force.
02
The teacher        us to hand in the essay a day early — she asked nicely and we agreed.
AnswergotTook asking/persuading.
03
My parents never        me stay out past midnight, even as a teenager.
AnswerletPermission, negated.
04
I usually        the mechanic check my brakes every six months.
AnswerhaveIt's his role — arranged.
05
My sister        me study for the exam by testing me with flashcards.
AnswerhelpedAssistance, working together.
06
The manager        everyone work the holiday shift — nobody had a choice.
AnswermadeNo choice — force.
07
After a long conversation, we finally        him to reconsider his decision.
AnswergotTook real persuading.
08
My boss will        the new intern shadow me for the first week.
AnswerhaveArranging a role/duty.
09
The rain        the outdoor wedding move indoors at the last minute.
AnswermadeForce — no choice.
10
Will your parents        you travel alone next year?
AnswerletAsking about permission.
11
The coach        the team practise twice as hard after the loss.
AnswermadeForce — a demand.
12

AnswerhadArranged for a favor.
13

AnswermadeMake + object + adjective.

04Fix the Structure10 items

Some sentences have exactly one structural mistake. Some are already correct.

worked example
My father made me to clean my room.
make never takes to — drop it.
→ My father made me clean my room.

Not every sentence has a mistake — some are already correct. Decide first, then fix only what needs it.
26
My father made me to clean my room every Saturday.
AnswerMy father made me clean my room every Saturday.make never takes to.
27
I got my brother fix my laptop last week.
AnswerI got my brother to fix my laptop last week.get always needs to.
28
The teacher let us to leave ten minutes early.
AnswerThe teacher let us leave ten minutes early.let never takes to.
29
I had the mechanic to check my car.
AnswerI had the mechanic check my car.have (active) never takes to.
30
She helped me to organize my closet last weekend.
AnswerNo error — correct as it is.Correct — help can take to or the base verb.
31
They made the interns to work through lunch.
AnswerThey made the interns work through lunch.make never takes to.
32
He got the landlord fixing the leak within a week.
AnswerHe got the landlord to fix the leak within a week.get always needs to + verb, not -ing.
33
We let the kids to choose the movie tonight.
AnswerWe let the kids choose the movie tonight.let never takes to.
34
I'll have my assistant to confirm the appointment.
AnswerI'll have my assistant confirm the appointment.have (active) never takes to.
35
My friend helped me move the boxes last weekend.
AnswerNo error — correct as it is.Correct — help without to is just as natural.
