01Three Neighboursreference
structure
make + object + adjective / noun / base verb
Three things can sit after the object, and each one says something different. An adjective describes what the object became: the news made her happy. A noun gives it a new identity: they made her captain. A base verb says the object was forced to act: she made me wait.
adjectiveThe news made her happy.what she became
nounThey made her captain.a new identity or role
base verb · no toShe made me wait.forced to act
