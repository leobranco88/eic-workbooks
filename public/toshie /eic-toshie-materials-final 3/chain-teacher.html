The rules
01
The cue gives you the material — you build the sentence.Each cue is a collocation: an object and the adjective it takes. Nothing else is given.
02
The adjective is the bridge.The adjective that closes one link opens the next: slippery → the slippery streets made…
03
Every link is make + object + adjective.No other verb. No because, no so. One sentence per link.
04
The chain ends on me.However long the road, the last link arrives at the speaker.
05
Change the tense, and it's a new chain.Same cues, same objects and adjectives — but the verb form is the real challenge now.
Adjectives that break the chain
afraid→frightened
alone→lonely
asleep→sleeping
ill→sick
glad→happy
upset→unhappy
These sit after the verb but never before a noun. The afraid children is impossible — the chain has to swap them out.
