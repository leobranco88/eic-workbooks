2.

PRÁTICA · E Formal ou Informal? His or Him? 10 items
Lembrando: as duas formas estão certas gramaticalmente — a diferença é o registro. O possessivo (his, her, their, Rio's) soa mais formal e é mais comum na escrita cuidadosa (e-mails, cartas, relatórios). O pronome objeto (him, her, them, Rio) soa mais natural e é o que se ouve na fala e em mensagens do dia a dia.

Nenhuma dica desta vez — decida pelo tom e pelas palavras da própria frase. Clique no número pra conferir.

1.
Honestly, I don't mind him tagging along. (him / his)
2.
The committee objected to their changing the policy without notice. (their / them)
3.
We would appreciate his confirming the refund in writing. (his / him)
4.
Thanks for you helping out today, seriously. (your / you)
5.
I was surprised at the manager's failing to respond to three emails. (the manager's / the manager)
6.
I get why you're annoyed about him canceling last minute. (his / him)
7.
I'm not bothered about them coming late, don't worry about it. (their / them)
8.
We are grateful for their supporting this project from the start. (their / them)
9.
Can you believe Sena forgetting the tickets again? (Sena's / Sena)
10.
The hotel thanked us for our choosing to stay again. (our / us)
