02 Gerund Check 12 items

Right after a preposition, the verb always takes the -ING form. No exceptions.

verb bank
check · confirm · eat · lead · learn · miss · organize · pay · retire · solve · wait · change
The verbs are in base form and NOT in order.
1.
He's not keen on waiting at the gate for hours.
2.
They objected to paying extra for luggage.
3.
She's committed to learning ten new words a day.
4.
I'm slowly getting accustomed to eating dinner so late.
5.
He reacted well to changing his seat at the last minute.
6.
The guide is familiar with leading tours in three languages.
7.
She copes with missing a flight better than most people.
8.
The hotel insisted on confirming the booking by phone.
9.
I'm tired of checking my phone for updates.
10.
They dream of retiring somewhere quiet by the sea.
11.
He's capable of solving problems no one else can.
12.
We're participating in organizing next year's trip.
03 Ask the Question 8 items

Turn each statement into a question. Keep the preposition at the end.

1. Mika is excited about her new apartment.
Model questionWhat is Mika excited about?
2. He suffers from terrible motion sickness on boats.
Model questionWhat does he suffer from?
3. Bad weather prevented them from hiking that day.
Model questionWhat did bad weather prevent them from doing?
4. She recovered from the surgery faster than expected.
Model questionWhat did she recover from?
5. The whole neighborhood got involved in the festival.
Model questionWhat did the neighborhood get involved in?
6. The airline accused the passenger of causing a delay.
Model questionWhat did the airline accuse the passenger of?
7. He's not very keen on spicy food.
Model questionWhat is he not keen on?
8. She's committed to saving for a house.
Model questionWhat is she committed to?
