The Wrong Room

READ The Wrong Room

Every bold sentence uses a verb or adjective + preposition from this round — all new vocabulary. Find each one — you'll turn them into questions in Section 02.

Sena arrived at the hotel very late. She was tired after a long flight. She gave her name at the desk. The receptionist looked at the screen, then looked again. The receptionist was confused about1 the reservation.

The reservation was under a different name — Rio Alencar. Rio was Sena's business partner. He had booked the trip for both of them, but only his name was in the system. Sena stayed calm. She insisted on2 getting a room immediately.

Sena had to wait for the manager. Ten minutes passed. Then twenty. She was tired of3 waiting. Finally, the manager arrived with a room — but not the one she had booked. It was smaller, on the third floor, with no view. Sena wasn't keen on4 the room at all.

Sena agreed to stay in the small room for one night. The manager promised to fix the reservation before Rio arrived. Sena was excited about5 seeing Rio the next day. She believed the manager, and finally went to sleep.

Sena woke up early and went down to the front desk before breakfast. The manager smiled and said the reservation was already fixed. Sena had stayed calm all night, even in a small room with no view. She felt capable of6 handling anything after that.

That afternoon, Rio finally arrived at the hotel. He found Sena in the lobby and apologized right away. He admitted he'd been involved in7 the mistake with the booking. Sena laughed and told him not to worry. She was not angry with8 him — the trip was only just beginning.

Glossário · vocabulário novo desta unidade no texto
1. confused about — não entender algo claramente · confuso(a) com · 〜について混乱している
2. insist on — recusar-se a aceitar "não" · insistir em · 〜を主張する
3. tired of — cansado(a) de algo repetitivo ou longo · cansado(a) de · 〜にうんざりしている
4. keen on — animado(a), interessado(a) em algo · animado(a)/interessado(a) em · 〜に熱心
5. excited about — animado(a), esperando algo bom · animado(a) com · 〜にわくわくする
6. capable of — ter a habilidade de fazer algo · capaz de · 〜する能力がある
7. involved in — ter participação em algo, envolvido(a) · envolvido(a) em · 〜に関わっている
8. angry with (someone) — bravo(a) com uma pessoa · bravo(a) com · （人）に怒っている
02 Turn It Into a Question 8 items

The underlined part is the information you're asking about — that's what becomes "what" in your question. When you see the + doing tag, the underlined part is an action, so your question needs to end in "doing" (e.g. "What did she insist on doing?").

1. "The receptionist was confused about the reservation."
Model questionWhat was the receptionist confused about?
2. "She insisted on getting a room immediately." + doing
Model questionWhat did Sena insist on doing?
3. "She was tired of waiting."
Model questionWhat was she tired of?
4. "Sena wasn't keen on the room."
Model questionWhat wasn't Sena keen on?
5. "Sena was excited about seeing Rio the next day." + doing
Model questionWhat was Sena excited about doing?
6. "She felt capable of handling anything after that." + doing
Model questionWhat did Sena feel capable of doing?
7. "He'd been involved in the mistake with the booking."
Model questionWhat had Rio been involved in?
8. "She was not angry with him."
Model questionWho was Sena not angry with?
03 Check What Happened 6 items

Comprehension questions. The answer is always in the text.

1. Why was the receptionist confused when Sena arrived?
AnswerBecause the reservation was under a different name — Rio's, not Sena's.
2. Who is Rio, and what does he have to do with the mix-up?
AnswerRio is Sena's business partner. He booked the trip for both of them, but only his name went into the system.
3. What did Sena insist on before agreeing to the smaller room?
AnswerShe insisted on getting a room immediately, and on the reservation being fixed before Rio arrived.
4. Did Sena get what she originally booked? How do you know?
AnswerNo — the text says she got a smaller room, on the third floor, with no view, and that she wasn't keen on it.
5. How did Sena feel about herself the next morning?
AnswerShe felt capable of handling anything after that.
6. What did Rio say when he arrived? Was Sena angry with him?
AnswerHe admitted he'd been involved in the mistake with the booking. No — Sena was not angry with him.
04 Speaking Corner talk with your teacher

Use at least three dependent prepositions from this round while you talk.

Has something like this ever happened to you — a booking mix-up, a wrong room? What were you confused about in the moment?
Think of a place you visited that you weren't keen on at first. What changed your mind, if anything?
Are you the kind of traveler who insists on getting exactly what was booked, or the kind who copes with whatever works out?
Think of a time something went wrong and you were involved in the mistake — even a small part of it. Were you angry with yourself?
