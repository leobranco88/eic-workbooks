EXTRA Turn the Underlined Part Into a Question 10 items · who · what · where

A dependent preposition always connects a verb or adjective to a person, a thing, or a place — never to a time or a reason. That's why this set only uses who, what, and where. Decide which one fits, based on what the underlined part actually is.

Example
whoThe receptionist eventually apologized to the hotel manager for the mix-up.
Think: "the hotel manager" is a person, so the question starts with Who. And "apologize" is always followed by "to" — a genuine dependent preposition — so "to" stays at the end.
→ Who did the receptionist eventually apologize to?
