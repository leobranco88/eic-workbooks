Dependent Prepositions
— The -ing Addendum

Prática direta: pronome antes do gerúndio, gerúndio negativo, e transformar frases em pergunta. Sem leitura — já sabe a regra? Então mostra.

PRÁTICA · A Complete com o pronome certo 12 items

Escreva a forma (possessivo ou pronome objeto) que falta antes do gerúndio. Clique no número pra conferir.

1.
Sena was surprised at him / his apologizing so quickly. (he)
2.
Rio wasn't happy about their / them forgetting the booking. (they)
3.
The manager insisted on my / me waiting in the lobby. (I)
4.
Sena complained about our / us being placed in the wrong room. (we)
5.
Rio's mother was worried about her traveling alone at night. (she)
6.
The whole staff was excited about Rio's / Rio joining the trip. (Rio)
7.
The receptionist didn't mind Sena's / Sena asking so many questions. (Sena)
8.
Toshie was used to their / them changing the itinerary at the last minute. (they)
9.
I'm tired of your / you canceling plans at the last minute. (you)
10.
She was proud of its / it turning out so well. (it, referring to the trip)
11.
We were all surprised at her leaving so suddenly. (she)
12.
Sena looked forward to our / us traveling together again next year. (we)
PRÁTICA · B Deixe o gerúndio negativo 12 items

Traduza usando preposição + not + gerúndio. Clique no número pra conferir.

1. Ele está com medo de não conseguir o quarto a tempo.彼は時間内に部屋を確保できないのではないかと心配している。
AnswerHe's afraid of not getting the room in time.
2. Ela está preocupada em não encontrar as malas no aeroporto.彼女は空港でスーツケースが見つからないのではないかと心配している。
AnswerShe's worried about not finding the luggage at the airport.
3. Ela está cansada de não ser levada a sério pelo hotel.彼女はホテルに真剣に受け止めてもらえないことにうんざりしている。
AnswerShe's tired of not being taken seriously by the hotel.
4. Eles têm orgulho de não terem desistido da viagem.彼らは旅行を諦めなかったことを誇りに思っている。
AnswerThey're proud of not giving up on the trip.
5. Sena está com medo de não fazer amigos na cidade nova.セナは新しい街で友達ができないのではないかと心配している。
AnswerSena is afraid of not making friends in the new city.
6. Ele reclamou de não ser avisado sobre a troca de quarto.彼は部屋の変更について知らされなかったことに文句を言った。
AnswerHe complained about not being told about the room change.
7. Ela ainda não se acostumou a não ter o wi-fi funcionando.彼女はまだWi-Fiが使えないことに慣れていない。
AnswerShe still isn't accustomed to not having the wifi working.
8. Eu confio nele para não desistir no meio da viagem.私は彼が旅の途中で諦めないと信じている。
AnswerI rely on him not giving up halfway through the trip.
9. Ela vive pensando em não ter aceitado aquele quarto.彼女はあの部屋を受け入れなかったことについて、ずっと考え続けている。
AnswerShe keeps thinking about not having accepted that room.
10. Ele é bom em não deixar transparecer o nervosismo.彼は緊張を表に出さないことが得意だ。
AnswerHe's good at not showing his nervousness.
11. Eles são capazes de não desistir mesmo nas piores situações.彼らは最悪の状況でも諦めない能力がある。
AnswerThey're capable of not giving up even in the worst situations.
12. Ela está animada por não ter que acordar cedo amanhã.彼女は明日早起きしなくていいことにわくわくしている。
AnswerShe's excited about not having to wake up early tomorrow.
PRÁTICA · C Turn It Into a Question 8 items

Cada frase esconde uma pessoa ou uma coisa depois da preposição — às vezes é quem faz a ação do gerúndio, às vezes é a situação inteira. Decida se é who ou what e monte a pergunta. Clique no número pra conferir.

1. Sena was surprised at Rio insisting on paying for the taxi.
Model questionWho was Sena surprised at?
2. The whole team was excited about Toshie joining the trip.
Model questionWho was the whole team excited about?
3. Rio's mother was worried about him traveling alone.
Model questionWho was Rio's mother worried about?
4. The manager didn't mind Sena asking so many questions.
Model questionWho didn't the manager mind?
5. Everyone was impressed by the guide handling the group so well.
Model questionWho was everyone impressed by?
6. Sena was tired of hotels forgetting her reservation.
Model questionWhat was Sena tired of?
7. Rio was proud of not losing his temper.
Model questionWhat was Rio proud of?
8. The whole group was confident about Sena not overpacking this time.
Model questionWho was the whole group confident about?
