Reading the Fine Print

Renata's team follows Valore Bank's Code of Conduct on conflicts of interest. What feels fair in an interview and what the policy actually says are sometimes two different things. Read the policy once, then judge six real cases the way you'd have to.

Before You Start · A New Kind of Case
Listen to the brief
0:00 0:00

The reality: Navigating an organization politically isn't a character flaw. One widely cited study found that 93% of managers report politics exist in their workplace, and 70% believe engaging with it is necessary to succeed. Political awareness is a skill worth building, not something to feel guilty about.

Your job: HR sits on the other side of that same reality. Industry research notes that HR teams are at the center of compliance, responsible for policy design and for making sure ethical standards are followed in practice, not just written down. A policy only works if what happens on the ground actually matches what it says on paper.

This lesson works differently from the other ones in this series. You'll interview an employee, hear their side, then apply the actual written policy, not just what seems reasonable. Once you've reached a verdict, you'll draft how you'd report it to Renata, clearly and without softening the outcome. This is the technical half of the job: the soft skills you've practiced only work if the interpretation underneath them is right.

Sources: Gandz, J., & Murray, V. V. (1980). "The Experience of Workplace Politics." Academy of Management Journal, 23(2). · Society for Human Resource Management (SHRM), "Employment Law & Compliance." · CoAdvantage, "Managing HR Compliance: A Comprehensive Guide for Employers" (2026).

01The Policy

This is the actual language from Valore Bank's Code of Conduct. Read it once in full. If a section feels dense, click "Plain English" to see it broken down, with a real example.

VALORE BANK
Human Resources & Compliance
Document COC-4.2
Effective January 1, 2026
Owner Compliance Department
Code of Conduct: Conflicts of Interest & Outside Activities

This policy applies to all Valore Bank employees and independent contractors (PJ) engaged under a services agreement. It governs gifts, outside employment, personal relationships, financial interests, confidentiality, and the reporting obligations that follow from each.

Section 1
Gifts & Business Entertainment

Employees may accept business gifts, meals, or entertainment from clients, vendors, or business partners only when they are of reasonable and customary value, not exceeding $150 per instance, and directly connected to a legitimate business purpose. Any gift or entertainment exceeding this limite, patamarO ponto ou valor a partir do qual uma regra diferente passa a valer.Other ways to say this: limit, cutoff point, or any gift of cash or a cash equivalent, must be declined or, if declining is not practical, disclosed to and approved by the employee's manager and the Compliance department within five business days of receipt.

Section 2
Outside Employment & Moonlighting

Employees must obtain prior written approval from their manager and Human Resources before engaging in outside employment, consulting, or business activities, whether compensated or not, if such activity could reasonably compete with, or create a conflict with, the employee's duties at Valore Bank. Failure to disclose outside employment prior to iniciar, começarForma formal de dizer "começar", comum em contratos e documentos legais.Other ways to say this: starting, beginning it constitutes a violation of this policy, regardless of whether an actual conflict later materializes.

Section 3
Hiring & Supervising Relatives

No employee may participate in the hiring, supervision, performance evaluation, or compensation decisions of a family member or someone with whom they have a close personal relationship. Where such a relationship exists or arises, the employee must disclose it to Human Resources immediately, and linhas de subordinação, hierarquiaA estrutura que define quem se reporta a quem dentro de uma empresa.Other ways to say this: chain of command, hierarchy will be adjusted to remove the conflict.

Section 4
Personal Trading & Financial Interests

Employees with access to relevante, significativoAqui não significa "material" físico. Significa algo importante o suficiente pra afetar uma decisão.Other ways to say this: significant, important non-public information about a client or contraparteA outra parte envolvida em uma transação ou negociação financeira.Other ways to say this: the other party, trading partner are prohibited from trading in that company's valores mobiliários, títulosInstrumentos financeiros negociáveis, como ações ou títulos. Não tem relação com "segurança".Other ways to say this: stocks, shares, financial instruments, and must disclose any pre-existing financial interest in a company under active engagement with Valore Bank to Compliance before any related work begins.

Section 5
Confidential Information

Employees must not disclose confidential, de propriedade exclusiva, confidencialInformação ou tecnologia que pertence exclusivamente a uma empresa e não pode ser compartilhada.Other ways to say this: confidential, exclusive, or non-public information belonging to Valore Bank or its clients to any third party, including friends, family, or professional contacts at other institutions, regardless of the employee's intent or the perceived harmlessness of the disclosure.

Section 6
Reporting Obligations & Deadlines

Any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest must be disclosed to Compliance within five business days of the employee becoming aware of it. Late disclosure, even of a conflict that is ultimately approved, is treated as a separate compliance failure.

Section 7
Contractor Exclusivity & Time Commitment

Independent contractors engaged under a services agreement (PJ) are not employees under CLT, but are expected to dedicate the full contracted hours exclusively to Valore Bank's work during those hours. A contractor found performing other paid work, including personal projects, freelance clients, or side businesses, during hours billed to Valore Bank is in violação de contratoQuando uma das partes não cumpre o que foi combinado em um contrato.Other ways to say this: contract violation, breaking an agreement, independent of whether the other work is confidential, competitive, or related to banking in any way. This is treated as a conflict of commitment, and is addressed under the individual service agreement rather than under Sections 1 through 6 of this Code.

Approved by Valore Bank Compliance Department

02The Language

Eight terms you'll need to read a conflict-of-interest case correctly, and to write it up the way Compliance expects.

1

An employee finds out her brother-in-law applied for a role on her team. Before anything moves forward, she emails HR to explain the relationship and asks to be removed from the hiring process. She did the right thing: she chose to declarar, revelarInformar oficialmente algo relevante (como um conflito de interesse) para a empresa, antes que se torne um problema.Other ways to say this: report, declare, come forward with the relationship immediately.

Think about it
Why does disclosing a conflict early usually protect an employee more than staying quiet and hoping it doesn't matter?
Model answer

Early disclosure shows good judgment and lets the company fix the situation before any harm happens. Staying quiet turns a manageable conflict into a policy violation the moment it's discovered.

Your call, reporting this to Renata
Solid moveShe followed Section 3 exactly: disclosed immediately and stepped back before any decision was made. This is the policy working as intended.
RiskyPunishing someone for correctly disclosing a conflict teaches the whole team to stop disclosing early next time.
RiskyEven a compliant case needs a short record on file, so there's no confusion about who decided what if the relative is later hired by someone else.
2

An analyst spends weekends building websites for small businesses. He never mentioned it to anyone, assuming it was "just a hobby that happens to pay." That's trabalhar em outro emprego (geralmente escondido)Ter um segundo trabalho, remunerado ou não, geralmente fora do horário normal, sem o conhecimento formal do empregador principal.Other ways to say this: side job, freelancing on the side, working a second gig, whether he thinks of it that way or not.

Think about it
Why does Section 2 care about the disclosure itself, and not just whether the outside work actually competes with the bank?
Model answer

Without disclosure, nobody can actually check whether it competes or not. The rule protects the bank from conflicts nobody was ever able to evaluate in the first place.

Your call, reporting this to Renata
Solid moveSection 2 is clear: not disclosing before starting is the violation itself, separate from whether the work turns out to be harmless.
RiskyJudging the outside work as harmless skips the actual rule, which requires disclosure before starting, not after the fact.
RiskyQuietly closing the matter leaves no record, and gives the impression that undisclosed side work is fine as long as no one asks.
3

A manager wants to hire a family friend, but insists it's fine because the offer reflects nepotismoFavorecer parentes ou pessoas próximas em decisões de contratação ou promoção, independentemente do mérito.Other ways to say this: favoritism, cronyism-free, em condições de mercado, sem favorecimentoUma decisão ou transação feita como se as partes não tivessem relação pessoal alguma, sem tratamento especial.Other ways to say this: at market terms, without favoritism market terms: same salary band, same interview process as everyone else.

Think about it
Does treating the hire fairly on paper remove the conflict described in Section 3?
Model answer

No. Section 3 isn't only about fairness in the offer, it's about who makes the decision. As long as the manager personally participates in hiring someone close to them, the conflict exists regardless of how fair the terms look.

Your call, reporting this to Renata
Solid moveThis follows Section 3 precisely: the relationship must be disclosed and the decision moved to someone without a personal connection to the candidate.
RiskyFair terms don't fix the actual problem: the manager is still the one deciding, which is exactly what the policy prohibits.
RiskyThe policy doesn't ban hiring someone close to an employee, it just requires someone else to make that decision.
4

A vendor offers a procurement manager a "referral bonus" for choosing their firm again next year, paid quietly and por fora, não registradoAlgo feito sem registro oficial, geralmente pra esconder de auditoria ou impostos.Other ways to say this: unofficially, under the table. Everyone recognizes this for what it is: a propina, comissão irregularUm pagamento secreto e ilegítimo dado a alguém em troca de favorecer quem paga em uma decisão de negócio.Other ways to say this: bribe, under-the-table payment, not a gift.

Think about it
What separates a kickback from the kind of gift Section 1 actually allows?
Model answer

A kickback is tied directly to a specific decision in the giver's favor, and is deliberately hidden. A permitted gift has no strings attached to a decision and is disclosed openly if it's large.

Your call, reporting this to Renata
Solid moveA kickback tied to a specific decision and paid off the books goes well beyond a late disclosure. This needs immediate escalation, not a Section 1 gift review.
RiskyComparing this to an ordinary gift misses the part that actually matters: it was paid specifically to influence a decision, in secret.
RiskyQuietly returning the money treats a serious violation like a paperwork fix, and leaves no record for Compliance to act on.
5

A junior banker tells his manager, in a private conversation, exactly what he thinks the client should do, hoping to seem impressive later if it works out. Nobody asked him to opinar, se manifestarDar sua opinião sobre um assunto, geralmente quando não era esperado que a pessoa participasse.Other ways to say this: give an opinion, chime in this way, and the manager feels a strange, influência indevidaPressão ou tentativa de influenciar uma decisão de forma inadequada, fora dos canais apropriados.Other ways to say this: inappropriate pressure, improper influence kind of pressure to agree, even without a clear conflict of interest.

Think about it
Why might undue influence still be worth naming, even when it doesn't match any single numbered section of the policy?
Model answer

Not every problem fits a numbered rule perfectly. Naming the pattern, someone pushing a decision through channels they weren't asked to use, still matters for how decisions get made fairly.

Your call, reporting this to Renata
Solid moveNot every real issue maps to a numbered section. Naming what's happening gives Renata the chance to address it before it becomes a habit.
RiskyConcluding there's no issue just because no section applies misses the actual behavior worth flagging.
RiskyIgnoring the junior banker doesn't address the pattern, and can push the same behavior into quieter, harder-to-see channels.
6

An employee discloses a side consulting arrangement, correctly and in writing, but three months after it started rather than within the five-day window. The arrangement itself turns out to be entirely harmless.

Think about it
Why does Section 6 treat a late disclosure as its own violation, even when the underlying conflict is approved?
Model answer

The deadline exists so the company can review a conflict before, not after, it's already been running for months. A late disclosure anula o propósitoQuando uma ação acaba invalidando a própria razão de existir de uma regra.Other ways to say this: undermines the point, makes it pointless even if the answer would have been yes.

Your call, reporting this to Renata
Solid moveSection 6 treats the two facts separately on purpose. Reporting both keeps the record accurate without over-punishing a harmless conflict.
RiskyCalling this fully compliant erases the late disclosure, which the policy explicitly treats as its own failure.
RiskyA three-month delay is a real issue, but treating it as a firing offense is far more severe than the violation itself calls for.
7

A PJ developer is contracted for 40 horas faturáveisAs horas de trabalho que um prestador de serviço cobra formalmente do cliente contratante.Other ways to say this: chargeable hours, invoiced hours a week on the mobile app team. During a routine review, his manager notices GitHub commits to an unrelated personal project during hours billed to Valore Bank. Nothing confidential was shared, nothing competes with the bank. But it's still a conflito de comprometimentoDiferente de conflito de interesse, aqui o problema não é lealdade ou sigilo, é o tempo já contratado não estar sendo dedicado ao cliente que pagou por ele.Other ways to say this: time-commitment issue, divided attention: the billed time simply isn't going where it's supposed to.

Think about it
How is a conflict of commitment different from the kinds of conflicts covered in Sections 1 through 6?
Model answer

Sections 1 through 6 are about loyalty, secrecy, or fairness, whether someone might act against the company's interest. A conflict of commitment is simpler: it's about whether someone is actually spending the paid time they agreed to.

Your call, reporting this to Renata
Solid moveThis keeps the record accurate: it's a contract time issue, not a loyalty or confidentiality issue, so it belongs under his services agreement, not Sections 1 through 6.
RiskyCalling this a conflict of interest mixes two different problems. Nothing suggests he'd benefit personally at the bank's expense, the issue is simply billed time not being used as agreed.
RiskyWhether the side project competes with the bank isn't the point here. The bank paid for hours it didn't actually get.
8

Two PJ contractors compare notes at a meetup and realize they've both been billing full-time hours to Valore Bank and to a separate client, for the exact same block of hours each week. One of them, laughing, calls it "efficient." There's no cláusula de exclusividadeUma cláusula contratual que exige que o prestador de serviço trabalhe exclusivamente para aquele cliente durante o período contratado.Other ways to say this: exclusivity requirement, non-compete term being tested here, it's simpler and worse than that: it's cobrar duas vezes pela mesma coisaReceber pagamento de duas fontes diferentes pelas mesmas horas de trabalho, geralmente sem que nenhuma das partes saiba da outra.Other ways to say this: billing fraud, double-billing, getting paid twice for hours that only existed once.

Think about it
Why is double-dipping considered more serious than an ordinary side project during work hours?
Model answer

An ordinary side project takes time away from the client without necessarily involving deception about billing. Double-dipping actively bills two clients for the same hours, which is closer to fraud than to a simple time-management problem.

Your call, reporting this to Renata
Solid moveBilling two clients for the identical hours goes beyond a time-commitment problem, since it involves actively deturpar, representar de forma falsaDescrever algo de um jeito que não é verdadeiro, geralmente pra enganar.Other ways to say this: distorting, falsely representing hours worked to at least one of them.
RiskyTreating this the same as a regular side-project case understates what's actually happening: money was collected twice for time that only existed once.
RiskyLetting them simply choose going forward doesn't address the hours already billed fraudulently in the past.

04Your Reality

The policy gives you the rule. Reporting it well is a separate skill, one that decides whether Renata can actually act on what you found.

Discussion 01

Why is it important to report a compliant case with the same clarity as a violation, instead of only writing detailed reports when something went wrong?

Model answer

A clear record of compliant cases shows the policy working, protects the employee from future doubt, and gives leadership an accurate picture instead of one that only shows problems.

Discussion 02

What is lost if an advisor softens a violation in the report to make the conversation with leadership easier?

Model answer

Leadership ends up making a decision based on a smaller problem than the one that actually exists, and any consequence that follows will feel disproportionate to what they were told.

Discussion 03

How does interviewing an employee directly change what an advisor can report, compared to only reading a policy document?

Model answer

The policy tells you what should happen. The interview tells you what actually happened, including context, timing, and intent, which is often exactly what determines how a case gets classified.

Working through your own registered company instead of as a formal employee. More flexible, fewer guaranteed benefits.

"He's doing an internship in the marketing department while finishing his degree."

Employed by a third-party company but working on-site for another company.
"The cleaning staff are outsourced workers, employed by an outside facilities company."
Pejotização
Misclassification · disguised self-employment
Hiring someone as a PJ contractor specifically to avoid CLT benefits, even though the role functions like a formal employee.
"Labor courts have fined several companies for misclassifying employees as independent contractors purely to cut costs."

An extra month's pay required by law for CLT employees, usually split into two installments at year-end.
"Companies must pay the 13th-month salary in two installments, one in November and one in December."

A government-mandated fund employers contribute to monthly, mainly accessible if the employee is dismissed without cause.
