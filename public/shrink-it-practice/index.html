4Items 23 to 27
4

Shrink and join

Encolha uma das ideias até virar um nome e junte as duas frases com o marker.

Join the two sentences with the marker. Shrink one idea into a noun phrase.

Transforme em nome a ideia que vem depois do marker, sem sujeito e verbo: “The room was small” vira “the small room”. Quando o marker abre a frase, ponha uma vírgula depois do nome.

Example · exemplo resolvido

The elevator was broken. We took the stairs.

Join them with owing to.

Owing to the broken elevator, we took the stairs.

A ideia (the elevator was broken) virou um nome (the broken elevator). Com o marker no início, a vírgula separa o nome do resto da frase.

because ofwhy?

We are late because of the strike.

A noun after it. With a subject and a verb, use because.

due to / owing towhy? (formal)

The delay is due to a problem.

Writing and reports. Owing to is rare.

thanks togood reason

We get home faster thanks to the new subway line.

A good result. For a bad cause, because of.

despite / in spite ofeven so?

Despite the strike, we flew.

Despite has no “of”. In spite of has it. Both take a noun or an -ing word.

duringwhen?

Do not call during the movie.

A noun that marks a moment. How long? Use for: for two years.

The storm was strong → the strong storm

Take out the verb. A comma closes the noun when the marker opens the sentence.
