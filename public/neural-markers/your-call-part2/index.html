    How to play · como jogar

    1. Read the case.Leia a situação. Você é quem decide.
    2. Read the two advisors.Leia os dois conselheiros. São pessoas fictícias, com opiniões diferentes.
    3. Decide, with a Part 2 verb.Diga o que você faz: let, make, have, allow, require, ask, get…
    4. Explain, with a marker.Explique a sua decisão: because of, although, despite…
    5. Reveal the twist.Chegou uma informação nova. Mantenha ou mude a decisão, com outro marker.

    1. 01
      A key client wants a bigger discount.Um cliente importante quer um desconto maior.

      renovar o contratorenew the contract a margemthe margin um contrato mais longoa longer contract um compromisso de volumea volume commitment manter o preçohold the price abrir mão do negóciowalk away

      Decide I would let him have 5%, but I would make the client sign for two years. Although he is our biggest client, I cannot give 10% because of the margin.

      Twist The client says another supplier offered him 12%.

      Answer Even though the competitor offers 12%, I would not change my decision. I would have our technical team show him the difference in quality.

    2. 02
      A junior rep made a pricing mistake.Um vendedor júnior errou um preço.

      um erro de preçoa pricing mistake manter o preço combinadohonor the price assumir a responsabilidadetake responsibility perder dinheirolose money revisar uma cotaçãoreview a quote uma liçãoa lesson

      Decide I would have him call the client with me, and I would let him explain what happened. Despite the loss, I would make the company honor the price because of the relationship.

      Twist Finance says the loss is 15,000 reais and wants the rep to pay part of it.

      Answer I would not allow finance to charge him. Although the mistake was his, nobody reviewed the quote, so I would get the director to accept the loss.

    3. 03
      A new rep wants to lead a meeting alone.Um vendedor novo quer conduzir uma reunião sozinho.

      conduzir a reuniãolead the meeting dar apoio a alguémback someone up um cliente exigentea demanding client ensaiar a apresentaçãorehearse the presentation intervirstep in ganhar confiançabuild confidence

      Decide I would let him lead, but I would have him rehearse with me first. Because of the risk, I would ask him to send me the agenda, and I would join the meeting.

      Twist The client’s director just asked to join the meeting too.

      Answer Even though the director is coming, I would still let Bruno lead. I would only step in if something goes wrong.

    4. 04
      A client wants to test the product first.Um cliente quer testar o produto antes.

      fazer um testerun a test uma amostraa sample o prazothe deadline o laboratóriothe lab um atrasoa delay fechar o trimestreclose the quarter

      Decide I would let him test for four weeks. Although I need the order this quarter, a rushed test is risky. I would ask him to send me the results every week.

      Twist Your director says: “Get the order signed this quarter, or the target is gone.”

      Answer Despite the pressure, I would not push the client. I would get my director to accept the order for next quarter because of the long-term value.

    5. 05

      Twist The client says he wants to deal with only one person.

      Answer Even though both did good work, I would let the client choose. I would encourage the other rep to take a new account.

    6. 06
      A client wants 120 days to pay.Um cliente quer 120 dias para pagar.

      prazo de pagamentopayment terms fluxo de caixacash flow um limite de créditoa credit limit dividir o pagamentosplit the payment um pedido maiora bigger order receberget paid

      Decide I would let him pay in 90 days, but I would require him to increase the order by 20%. Due to our cash flow, I cannot go further.

      Twist The client says: “120 days, or we go to another supplier.”

      Answer Even though I want this order, I would not accept 120 days. I would get finance to offer a split payment instead.

    7. 07

      Twist He tells you he is thinking about leaving because he feels controlled.

      Answer Although I want him to be organized, I would let him choose his own system. In spite of my worry, I would ask him to show results in one month.
