Speak

Fale
17 items
1Ask your teacher the question.Faça a pergunta para o professor.
2Answer with a full sentence. Use the chunk at the end of the line.Responda com uma frase completa. Use o chunk no fim da linha.
3Ask one more question back.Faça mais uma pergunta de volta.
1.
Who inspired you to start learning English?inspire sb to
My teacher inspired me to start learning English.
2.
What does your job or school require everyone to do?require sb to
My company requires everyone to arrive by nine.
3.
Who do you trust to give you honest advice?trust sb to
I trust my sister to give me honest advice.
4.
Has anyone ever dared you to do something? What happened?dare sb to
My cousin dared me to eat something very spicy.
5.
What would you love for your family to do this year?would love (for) sb to
I’d love for my family to visit me in the summer.
6.
What can you hear people do on your street at night?hear sb do
I can hear people talk and laugh outside.
7.
Have you ever noticed a stranger do something strange? Tell me about it.notice sb do
Once I noticed a man talk to a tree.
8.
What is essential that a tourist do in Japan? Why?it’s essential that
It’s essential that tourists be quiet on the train.
9.
What did a doctor or a teacher advise you to do? Did you follow it?advise sb to
My doctor advised me to sleep more.
10.
What did someone recommend that you try? Was it a good idea?recommend that
A friend recommended that I try this restaurant.
11.
What does a school or a gym not permit people to do?permit sb to
My gym doesn’t permit people to take photos.
12.
What would you prefer for your friends to do on your birthday?would prefer (for) sb to
I’d prefer for my friends to come to my house.
13.
What did you help someone do this week?help sb (to)
I helped my neighbor carry some boxes.
14.
What is vital that a student do to learn a language?it’s vital that
It’s vital that a student practice every day.
15.
Did you arrange to meet someone this week? Tell me more.arrange to
I arranged to meet my friend on Saturday.
16.
Role play. You manage a small hotel. Tell a guest three rules.ask · require · request
We ask guests not to smoke. We require that guests leave the key at the desk. We request that guests be quiet after ten.
17.
Tell a story · 45 seconds. Tell me about a person who helped you. Who inspired you? What did they trust you to do?inspire · trust · help
My first teacher inspired me to love books. She trusted me to read aloud to the class.
