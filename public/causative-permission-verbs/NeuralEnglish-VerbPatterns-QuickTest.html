1 Quick review · one minute

Three families, one rule for the not

Revisão rápida
1
verb + sb + to
want · need · ask · tell · advise · remind · expect · allow
Not on the actionI told him not to call.
Not on the requestI didn’t tell him to call.
2
let · make · have + sb + verb
No “to”. The not goes on the first verb, always.
SayMy dad didn’t let me go.
NeverMy dad let me not go.
3
suggest · recommend · insist · demand + that + sb + verb
Formal. The verb stays in the base form for every pronoun.
SayI suggest that he not drive.
NeverI suggest that he doesn’t drive.
“that” não!

I want you tothat you go.

Eu quero que você vá. Em inglês: objeto + to.

don’t no meio?

He told me not todon’t call her.

Ele me disse pra não ligar pra ela. Depois da pessoa, o não é “not to”.

mesmas palavras, outra história

I didn’t ask Carla to leave.Eu não pedi pra Carla sair. Ninguém pediu nada.

I asked Carla not to leave.Eu pedi pra Carla não sair. O pedido foi “fica”.

The not on the first verb always needs an auxiliary.Don’t, doesn’t, didn’t, won’t. O verbo volta pra base: She didn’t ask, nunca didn’t asked.

The three families
1Family 1 · most verbs
Verb + object + to
want, need, ask, tell, advise, expect, allow, encourage, persuade, remind, warn, invite, convince, teach, force, would like
New: get, urge, beg, permit, require, enable, inspire, trust, dare, would love, would hate, would prefer, arrange (for sb to), help (sb or sb to)
Tell it apart: get = convenceu · have = função dela · make = sem escolha (only get takes to)
I want you to call her.Eu quero que você ligue pra ela.
not goes before to: I told him not to eat it.
See Family 1 in Part 2
2Family 2 · three verbs, no to
let · make · have + object + verb
let, make, have · have = aquilo é função da pessoa
New: see, hear, watch, feel, notice · help (sem to)
Let me carry that bag.Deixa eu carregar essa sacola.
See Family 2 in Part 2
3Family 3 · keeps that · formal
suggest · recommend + that + subject + verb
suggest, recommend, insist, demand, propose, request
New: it’s important / essential / vital that · advise, ask, urge, require (also Family 1)
Not allowed with sb + to: say → tell, explain → explain to me, suggest / recommend → that you
The teacher suggested that we read more.A professora sugeriu que a gente lesse mais.
Same bare verb for everyone, even he, she, it: that he go, that she be. Never goes, never is.
See Family 3 in Part 2
