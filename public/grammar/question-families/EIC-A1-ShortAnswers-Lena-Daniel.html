A resposta certa é mais curta do que você pensa

Em inglês, você não repete o verbo principal na resposta curta. O auxiliar faz todo o trabalho — e ele é sempre o mesmo da pergunta.

Como funciona

A regra da short answer

Três coisas para fixar antes de praticar.

Regra 1 · O auxiliar da pergunta = o auxiliar da resposta
Does Lena want to record a new episode? → Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Did Daniel work overtime last week? → Yes, he did. / No, he didn't.
Is Lena recording a podcast right now? → Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
Would Daniel like to take a cooking class? → Yes, he would. / No, he wouldn't.
Brazilian Trap · The most common mistakes
Does Lena want to grow her audience? — Yes, she wants.
→ Yes, she does.
Short answers use the auxiliary, never the main verb.
Did Daniel work overtime? — Yes, he worked.
→ Yes, he did.
After did, only did or didn't in the short answer.
Is she recording? — Yes, she is recording.
→ Yes, she is.
Short answers never repeat the main verb.
Regra 2 · Pronome substitui o sujeito
Does Lena need to edit a video?
sim Yes, she does.
não No, she doesn't.
Lena vira she. Repetir o nome funciona, mas o pronome é mais natural na conversa. Yes, Lena does é correto mas soa mais formal ou enfático.
Regra 3 · Sujeito composto vira "they"
Do Lena and Daniel work together?
sim Yes, they do.
não No, they don't.
Dois ou mais sujeitos = they. Nunca repita ambos os nomes na resposta curta.
Bônus · Extend the answer — short answers na conversa real
Does Lena want to collaborate with an artist?
Yes, she does — she's been talking to a DJ she really likes.
Did Daniel work overtime last week?
Yes, he did — the campaign launch was a total nightmare.
Is Lena trying to grow her audience?
Yes, she is — but it's taking longer than she expected.
Would Daniel like to change careers?
Yes, he would — he's thinking about going into food & beverage.
