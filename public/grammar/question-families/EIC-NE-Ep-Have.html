Have / Has.
Um verbo, dois caminhos.

Afirmativa, pergunta e short answer

Have e has seguem exatamente o mesmo sistema de do/does. O sujeito determina tudo: qual forma usar, como perguntar, e como responder. O caminho é fixo. O cérebro só precisa percorrê-lo rápido o suficiente.

O sistema completo

Um sujeito. Três decisões.

Identifique o sujeito e as três decisões abaixo se tornam automáticas.

I / you / we / they
They → have They have bags.
Do + they Do they have bags?
Yes, they do.
No, they don't.
he / she / it
She → has She has bags.
Does + she Does she have bags?
Yes, she does.
No, she doesn't.
Brazilian Trap · The 3 most common mistakes
Does she has a bag?
→ Does she have a bag?
After does/do, the verb always goes back to its base form.
Yes, she has.
→ Yes, she does.
Short answers use does or do. Never has or have.
Do she have a car?
→ Does she have a car?
she/he/it always take does, not do.
