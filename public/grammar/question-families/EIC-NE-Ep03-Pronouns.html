Brazilian Trap · The most common mistakes
The office? It have a small kitchen.
→ It has a small kitchen.
it/he/she → has, not have.
The desks are new. It was installed last month.
→ They were installed last month.
Plural subjects → they, not it.
Carol and Sam? He and she work together.
→ They work together.
Two people together → they, not he and she.
