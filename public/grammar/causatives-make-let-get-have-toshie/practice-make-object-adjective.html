01 The Pattern reference
structure
make + object pronoun + adjective
The adjective describes the object, not the subject. Get is what happens on its own — the room got warm. Make is what someone or something causes — the fire made the room warm.
The object pronoun goes between the two: never makes calmer her, always makes her calmer.

them彼らを・それらをeles, elas / os, as
