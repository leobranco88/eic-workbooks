01Five Verbs, Five Meaningsreference

Same idea — someone causes someone else to act — but each verb says something different about who wanted it.

MAKEforce · no choice
GETpersuade · takes effort
HAVEarrange · their role
HELPassist · together
LETallow · full choice
NO CHOICE FOR THE OBJECTFULL CHOICE FOR THE OBJECT
VerbStructureMeaningExample
MAKEmake + object + base verbforce — the object has no choiceMy mom made me clean my room.
LETlet + object + base verbpermission — the object has full choiceMy mom let me stay up late.
GETget + object + to + verbpersuade — it takes effort or convincingI got my brother to help me move.
HAVEhave + object + base verbarrange — it's their job or roleI had the mechanic fix my car.
HELPhelp + object + (to) + verbassist — you do it together; both forms are correctI helped him carry the boxes.
worked example
My daughter wanted to go to the concert, so I gave her permission.
1. Nuance: she wanted it, I allowed it → permission → let
2. Structure: let + object + base verb (no to)
→ I let her go to the concert.
02Which Verb Fits the Story?12 items

Read the scenario and write one full sentence with the causative verb that matches it.

01
My boss insisted, and I had no choice but to work the extra shift.
SuggestedMy boss made me work the extra shift.MAKE + object + base verb
02
The teacher wouldn't accept a messy essay, so we all had to rewrite ours.
SuggestedThe teacher made us rewrite our essays.MAKE + object + base verb
03
My father never allowed arguments — we simply had to apologise, whether we meant it or not.
SuggestedMy father made us apologise.MAKE + object + base verb
04
My parents agreed, and I was allowed to travel alone for the first time.
SuggestedMy parents let me travel alone.LET + object + base verb
05
The teacher didn't mind, so we were allowed to leave early.
SuggestedThe teacher let us leave early.LET + object + base verb
06
My manager agreed to my request, so now I'm allowed to work from home on Fridays.
SuggestedMy manager lets me work from home on Fridays.LET + object + base verb
07
My brother refused to help at first, but after a long conversation, he agreed.
SuggestedI got my brother to help me.GET + object + to + verb
08
The children didn't want to eat vegetables, but somehow their grandmother convinced them.
SuggestedTheir grandmother got the children to eat vegetables.GET + object + to + verb
09
I don't fix my own car — a professional does that for me, whenever I ask.
SuggestedI have the mechanic fix my car.HAVE + object + base verb
10
My assistant will contact the client on my behalf tomorrow — that's her job.
SuggestedI'll have my assistant contact the client tomorrow.HAVE + object + base verb
11
My neighbour and I moved the sofa together — I couldn't have done it alone.
SuggestedMy neighbour helped me move the sofa.HELP + object + base verb
12
My sister explained the grammar to me, step by step, until I understood.
SuggestedMy sister helped me understand the grammar.HELP + object + (to) + verb

04Change the Meaning8 items

Rewrite each sentence using the bracketed verb instead. The structure changes — and so does the meaning.

25
My father made me clean my room. [LET]
SuggestedMy father let me clean my room.Now she wanted to, and he simply allowed it — force becomes permission.
26
I let my daughter stay up late. [MAKE]
SuggestedI made my daughter stay up late.Now she didn't want to — permission becomes force.
27
I got my colleague to review the document. [HAVE]
SuggestedI had my colleague review the document.No persuasion needed now — it's simply his role.
28
I helped my brother paint the fence. [MAKE]
SuggestedI made my brother paint the fence.Now he's forced to do it alone — no longer a joint effort.
29
The teacher let the students leave early. [MAKE]
SuggestedThe teacher made the students leave early.Now they didn't want to go — permission becomes force.
30
I had the mechanic fix my car. [GET]
SuggestedI got the mechanic to fix my car.Now it implies he needed convincing — maybe he was busy or reluctant.
31
She made her assistant rewrite the email. [GET]
SuggestedShe got her assistant to rewrite the email.Softer — persuasion instead of a direct order.
32
My parents let me travel alone. [HELP]
SuggestedMy parents helped me travel alone.Completely different meaning — now they assisted, rather than just permitting it.
