Present Simple × Continuous Vic's Story Search
Normally vs. This week
Present Simple × Present Continuous Presente simples × presente contínuo
Present Simple
subject + verb (+s / +es for he, she, it) subject + don't / doesn't + verb Do / Does + subject + verb?
Kai reads.  ·  Bia reads. Kai doesn't read on weekends. Does Kai read every day?
Routines, habits, things that are generally true. Rotinas, hábitos, coisas geralmente verdadeiras.
Present Continuous
subject + am / is / are + verb-ing subject + am not / isn't / aren't + verb-ing Am / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing?
Kai is editing.  ·  They are rehearsing. Kai isn't editing right now. Is Kai editing a video?
Things happening now or temporary, this week / month. Coisas acontecendo agora ou temporárias, essa semana / mês.
