Right word test / quiz / exam Nothing wrong

Right word yes!Nothing wrong

Nos Estados Unidos, public school é escola do governo. No Reino Unido, é o contrário: escola particular.

6· Your turn

Now say it yourself

Três situações reais

  1. Say it out loud first.
  2. Use the chunks.Existem várias respostas certas.
  3. Write it on the lines.
ExampleAccording to the city, 40% of buses are late. One option would be a bus lane, but it would take sustained investment.
1

You write to the principal about one problem in your school. Give one number with its source, and one measure.

Escreva para o diretor sobre um problema da sua escola: um número com a fonte e uma medida.

Use according to · class size · one option would be

According to our own count, class size is 38 in the sixth grade. One option would be to split two classes, but it would take more teachers.

2

A friend says teachers are paid nothing. Agree with one part, compare fairly, and ask for the source.

Um amigo diz que professor ganha nada. Concorde com uma parte, compare com justiça e peça a fonte.

Use fair point · apples with oranges · could you share the source

Fair point, the pay is low in many cities. But let us not compare apples with oranges: the pay floor is the same, the cost of living is not. Could you share the source?

3

A visitor asks what learning poverty is, and why it is not the same as being out of school. Explain in three short sentences.

Um visitante pergunta o que é learning poverty e por que não é o mesmo que estar fora da escola. Explique em três frases.

Use learning poverty · out of school · compared with

Learning poverty means a ten-year-old cannot read a short text. A child can be in school and still be in learning poverty. Compared with children out of school, they are harder to see.
