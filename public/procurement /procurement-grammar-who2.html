WHO first —
or WHAT?

There is no wrong answer here. This is about one decision: what do you want your reader to process first? Read the full paragraph, feel the context, and choose. Then see what changes.

The question before every sentence
What do I want the reader to think about first? Who did it, or what happened?
Your answer shapes the sentence. Active puts WHO first. Passive puts WHAT first.
