Who's in
the Deal?

Before you read a Purchase Order or negotiate a contract, you need to know who the key people are — what they want, what they say, and why they matter in a procurement process.

Buyer — Althea Industrial
Supplier — Hua Li Components, Taiwan
The people involved
Buyer · Althea Industrial
Procurement Manager
Purchasing / Supply Chain
What they want
The best product at the best price — on time. They run the process from RFQ to Purchase Order, compare suppliers, negotiate terms, and own the supplier relationship. Their KPI is cost savings, lead time reduction, and zero stockouts.
Typical phrase
"We have three quotes on the table. Hua Li is 12% cheaper but their lead time is 60 days. GlobalParts is faster but we need to check their quality certification. Let's align before I send the PO."
RFQPurchase Orderlead timesupplier evaluationcost savings
Plant / Operations Manager
Production / Manufacturing
What they want
No stockouts. No production stops. They do not care about the price negotiation — they care about the result: materials arriving on time, in the right quantity, and to the right specification. They are the ones who feel the pain when a supplier fails to deliver.
Typical phrase
"I don't care which supplier you choose. What I need is 2,000 units of HX-44 on the floor by the 15th or Line 3 stops. Stockout right now costs us R$40k a day. Make it happen."
stockoutproduction stopsafety stocklead timeon-time delivery
Finance / Accounts Payable
Finance / Treasury
What they want
Correct documentation and predictable cash flow. They will only process a payment when the invoice matches the Purchase Order and the delivery receipt. A missing PO number or wrong quantity means the invoice goes back — and the supplier waits.
Typical phrase
"We cannot process this invoice. The amount does not match PO #BR-2024-0091. There is also no delivery receipt on file. Please resubmit with the correct documentation."
invoicepayment termscash flowdelivery receiptaccounts payable
Legal / Compliance
Legal Affairs
What they want
Protection. They review every clause before signing — looking for liability caps, penalty clauses, force majeure language, and governing law. They often slow things down — but they prevent bigger problems later.
Typical phrase
"We cannot sign the Purchase Agreement as written. Clause 11 has no liability cap and the governing law is Taiwan. We need both changed before this goes to management."
liability cappenalty clauseforce majeuregoverning lawPurchase Agreement
Supplier · Hua Li Components
Account Manager
Sales / Commercial — Hua Li Components, Taiwan
What they want
To win the RFQ and build a long-term relationship with Althea Industrial. They defend price, manage lead time expectations, and keep the buyer happy enough to come back for the next order. Their biggest challenge is competing on value — not just price.
Typical phrase
"I understand price is a concern. But our lead time is 30 days versus 60 from the competitor — and we hold ISO 9001 certification. The total cost of a stockout is much higher than our price difference."
RFQ responselead timeISO 9001total cost of ownershiprelationship management
Sales / Applications Engineer
Technical Sales — Hua Li Components, Taiwan
What they want
To confirm that their product meets the buyer's technical specifications. They review the RFQ, check tolerances, and confirm compatibility. They are the ones who say "yes, we can make it" — or "no, not with that spec".
Typical phrase
"I reviewed your technical specification for HX-44. The tolerance of ±0.05mm is achievable on our current line. However, the surface treatment you specified is non-standard — it will add 8 days to the lead time and USD 0.30 per unit."
specificationtolerancecompatibilitylead timenon-conformance
Logistics / Supply Chain Coordinator
Operations — Hua Li Components, Taiwan
What they want
To ship on time, with the right documents. They manage production scheduling, book freight, and prepare the shipping documents — Bill of Lading, packing list, commercial invoice. A mistake here can hold a shipment at customs for weeks.
Typical phrase
"We have confirmed vessel booking for 14 November, ETD Shanghai. The Bill of Lading and packing list will be sent within 24 hours of departure. Please confirm the Incoterm — our records show CIF Santos."
Bill of LadingETDpacking listIncotermcustoms clearance
Quality Manager
Quality Assurance — Hua Li Components, Taiwan
What they want
Zero non-conformances. They run inspection before shipment, issue certificates of conformance, and manage corrective actions when the buyer reports a defect. Their job is to make sure nothing leaves the factory that will come back as a complaint.
Typical phrase
"All units passed outgoing inspection against your technical specification Rev.3. Please find attached the certificate of conformance and inspection report for batch #HX-2024-114."

Reality check.

These questions are for discussion — in class or with yourself. There are no right answers.

In your company, who decides which supplier gets the order — procurement alone, or does operations or management have a say?
Think about the last time a supplier was chosen or changed. Who was in the room?
Have you ever had a payment held up because of a missing document — a wrong PO number, no delivery receipt, or an invoice that didn't match?
If yes: what was missing and how long did it take to resolve?
In your experience, does the legal team get involved in supplier contracts — or do procurement and management sign without legal review?
Think about the risks of signing without legal input. Which clause would you want them to check first?
Has a supplier ever missed a delivery deadline that caused a problem for your production or operations team?
What happened? Was there a penalty clause in the contract — and was it applied?

Collocations
Words that go together in procurement English
place
fazer / emitir
In procurement, "place" collocates with order-related nouns. It means to formally issue or submit something.
place + an order
fazer um pedido
Not: do an order
"We will place an order once the price is confirmed."
place + a Purchase Order
emitir uma ordem de compra
Not: open a Purchase Order
"Please place the Purchase Order before the supplier's deadline on Friday."
place + a bid
fazer uma oferta / lance
Not: put a bid
"Three suppliers were invited to place a bid for the HX-44 contract."
raise
emitir / levantar / escalar
"Raise" in procurement means to create a document or bring up a concern formally.
raise + a Purchase Order
emitir uma ordem de compra
Not: open a PO
"The system will not let me raise a PO without manager approval."
raise + a concern
levantar uma preocupação
Not: say a concern
"I need to raise a concern about the supplier's lead time before we sign."
raise + an invoice
emitir uma fatura
Not: do an invoice
"You can raise the invoice as soon as the delivery is confirmed."
meet
cumprir / atender
"Meet" means to successfully fulfil a requirement, standard, or deadline. Very common in procurement and quality.
meet + the deadline
cumprir o prazo
Not: respect the deadline
"The supplier confirmed they will meet the delivery deadline."
meet + the specification
atender à especificação
Not: follow the specification
"All units must meet the technical specification in Annex A."
meet + the MOQ
atingir o pedido mínimo
Not: arrive at the MOQ
"Our current demand does not meet the MOQ — we need to consolidate orders."
run
fazer / conduzir / rodar
"Run" collocates with processes and checks in a procurement and quality context.
run + an inspection
fazer uma inspeção
Not: make an inspection
"We always run an inspection before the goods leave the factory."
run + a supplier evaluation
conduzir uma avaliação de fornecedor
Not: do a supplier evaluation
"We run a supplier evaluation every six months to check on performance."
run + out of stock
ficar sem estoque
Not: finish stock
"If we don't reorder this week, we will run out of stock before the end of the month."
Procurement · 42 terms
Vocabulary
Documents & Process
penalty clause
penalidade contratual
A financial consequence applied when one party fails to meet the contract terms — such as late delivery or quality failure.
Documents & Process
payment terms
condições de pagamento
The agreed rules about when and how a buyer will pay the seller — for example, 30 days after the invoice date.
Documents & Process
force majeure
força maior
A contract provision that frees both parties from obligations when extraordinary events — such as disasters or conflicts — make fulfilment impossible.
Logistics & Operations
acknowledge
confirmar o recebimento / acusar o recibo
To formally confirm that a document was received and understood. 'Please acknowledge receipt of this PO.'
Grammar & Usage
supposed to
era para / devia
Fixed expression for plans, agreements, or expectations. Not for personal opinions.
""The delivery was supposed to arrive on Friday.""
Legal & Contracts
Non-conforming goods
Produtos Não Conformes
Goods that do not meet the specifications, quality standards, or requirements defined in the contract or purchase order. The buyer has the right to reject non-conforming goods and request replacement or a price reduction.
Grammar & Usage
must
deve / é obrigado a
Strong obligation — a rule, legal requirement, or non-negotiable condition.
""All invoices must include the PO number.""
Logistics & Operations
stockout
ruptura de estoque
When stock reaches zero and production or sales cannot continue. Every day of stockout has a direct financial cost.
Documents & Process
quotation
cotação / orçamento formal
A formal document from a supplier stating the price, quantity, and conditions under which they will sell their goods.
Logistics & Operations
resubmit
reenviar / submeter novamente
To send a corrected document again after an error was found. 'Please resubmit the invoice with the correct unit price.'
Legal & Contracts
Scope of supply
Escopo de Fornecimento
The precise definition of what the supplier is responsible for delivering — including goods, services, documentation, packaging, and installation if applicable. A well-defined scope prevents disputes about what was and was not included in the contract price.
Documents & Process
Incoterm
incoterm
Internationally recognised trade terms that define where responsibility transfers from seller to buyer, and who pays for shipping and insurance.
Logistics & Operations
vessel
navio / embarcação
A ship used for transporting cargo. 'We have confirmed vessel booking' = space on the ship is reserved.
Legal & Contracts
Arbitration
Arbitragem
A private dispute resolution process where a neutral third party (arbitrator) makes a binding decision instead of a court. Common in international contracts because it avoids the complexity of litigating across different legal systems. The ICC (International Chamber of Commerce) is a leading arbitration institution.
Documents & Process
clause
cláusula
A specific condition or requirement written into a contract or agreement.
Logistics & Operations
reorder
reabastecer / fazer novo pedido
To place a new purchase order for a product that is running low. 'We need to reorder HX-44 immediately.'
Quality & Compliance
compatibility
compatibilidade
Whether a component will work correctly with the buyer's existing equipment or system.
Quality & Compliance
inspection
inspeção
A physical check of goods — either at the factory before shipping (outgoing) or at the warehouse on arrival (incoming).
Logistics & Operations
invoice
fatura
A document from the supplier requesting payment for goods delivered. Must match the PO and GRN to be processed.
Legal & Contracts
Request for Quotation (RFQ)
Pedido de Cotação
A formal document sent by a buyer to one or more suppliers asking them to provide a price and delivery conditions for specific goods or services. It is not an order — it is a request for information.
Legal & Contracts
Confidentiality clause
Cláusula de Confidencialidade
A contract clause that prohibits one or both parties from disclosing sensitive information — such as pricing, technical specifications, or business plans — to third parties. Also known as an NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) when it is a standalone document.
Legal & Contracts
Liability cap
Limite de Responsabilidade
A contractual limit on the maximum financial compensation one party can claim from the other. Suppliers often propose low liability caps to protect themselves from large claims. Buyers should always check if the cap covers the full value of the order — and negotiate upward if necessary.
Logistics & Operations
freight
frete / carga
The goods being transported, or the cost of transporting them. 'Freight costs' = the price of shipping.
Grammar & Usage
should
deveria / acho que
Use for advice or personal opinion. Not for agreements or contracts.
""I think we should ask for better payment terms.""
Logistics & Operations
warehouse
armazém / depósito
The physical location where goods are stored after arrival and before being used in production or distributed.
Legal & Contracts
Net 30 / Net 60
Pagamento em 30 / 60 dias
Standard payment terms meaning the buyer must pay within 30 (or 60) days of the invoice date. Net 60 is common in B2B procurement and gives the buyer more time to manage cash flow. Suppliers may offer early payment discounts (e.g. 2/10 Net 30 = 2% discount if paid within 10 days).
Legal & Contracts
Governing law
Lei Aplicável / Foro
The jurisdiction whose laws will be used to interpret the contract and resolve disputes. In international procurement, this is critical — if a dispute goes to court orarbitrationA private dispute resolution process — a neutral arbitrator decides instead of a court. / Processo privado de resolução de disputas — um árbitro neutro decide em vez de um tribunal., the governing law determines the outcome. Always check with legal before accepting a foreign governing law.
Grammar & Usage
certificate of conformance
certificado de conformidade
A document from the supplier confirming goods meet the agreed specifications.
""Every batch is supposed to come with a certificate of conformance.""
Quality & Compliance
tolerance
tolerância
The acceptable range of variation in a measurement. E.g. ±0.05mm means the part can be 0.05mm bigger or smaller than the target.
Logistics & Operations
batch
lote
A group of products made in the same production run. Each batch has a unique reference for traceability — critical in chemicals and regulated industries.
Documents & Process
procurement
compras / suprimentos
The full process of finding, evaluating, and buying goods or services for a business.
Documents & Process
lead time
prazo de produção e entrega
The total time between placing an order and receiving the goods.
Logistics & Operations
specification
especificação técnica
The exact technical requirements for a product — dimensions, materials, purity, tolerances. The supplier must meet every point.
Documents & Process
MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)
quantidade mínima de pedido
The smallest quantity a supplier is willing to sell in a single order.
Documents & Process
Purchase Order (PO)
ordem de compra
A formal document issued by a buyer to a supplier, confirming the purchase of specific goods at an agreed price and quantity.
Documents & Process
purchase agreement
contrato de fornecimento
A formal legal document that defines the terms of an ongoing supply relationship between a buyer and a supplier.
Legal & Contracts
Time is of the essence
O prazo é essencial / fundamental
A legal phrase used in contracts meaning that meeting the deadline is a fundamental requirement, not a guideline. If a contract contains this phrase, a supplier who delivers even one day late can be heldin breach of contractWhen a party fails to fulfil a legal obligation under a contract. / Quando uma parte deixa de cumprir uma obrigação legal prevista em contrato..
Legal & Contracts
Warranty
Garantia
A supplier's formal commitment that goods will be free from defects for a defined period. The warranty period, start date (from delivery, installation, or first use), and what it covers are all negotiable and must be clearly specified in the contract.
Legal & Contracts
In breach of contract
Em descumprimento contratual
When a party fails to fulfil one or more of its contractual obligations. A breach can be minor (partial non-performance) or material (serious failure that entitles the other party to terminate and claim damages). Late delivery, non-conforming goods, and failure to pay are common examples.
Documents & Process
RFQ (Request for Quotation)
pedido de cotação formal
A document sent to suppliers asking them to provide a price and delivery details for specific goods or services.
Documents & Process
amendment
aditivo / alteração de contrato
A formal written change made to an existing contract or purchase order after it has been signed.
Grammar & Usage
Bill of Lading (B/L)
Conhecimento de Embarque
A legal shipping document required for customs clearance at the destination port.
""Hua Li was supposed to send the B/L within 24 hours of shipment.""
