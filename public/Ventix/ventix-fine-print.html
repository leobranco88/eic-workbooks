01The Policy

Leia uma vez com calma. As expressões destacadas em neon são as que decidem cada caso lá embaixo.

Ventix Compressors
Internal Policy · Section 4: Warranty Coverage & Claims

4.1 · Standard Warranty Period

New units are covered under warranty for fifteen months from the date of shipment, or twelve months from initial start-up, whichever comes first. Failures reported after this window fall outside standard coverage, unless the unit is enrolled in the Extended Warranty+ Plan.

4.2 · Preventive Maintenance & Record-Keeping

Coverage requires that maintenance be performed at the published intervals, using genuine Ventix parts and lubricants, with every service logged in the client's online maintenance record under the unit's serial number. A claim with missing or incomplete records can be denied on that basis alone, regardless of the actual cause of failure.

4.3 · Authorized Service Only

Any repair, inspection, or part replacement performed by a technician not certified by Ventix will void the warranty for that component, even if the work itself was performed correctly. This includes any non-genuine parts installed during that service.

4.4 · Operating Conditions

The warranty does not cover damage resulting from operation outside the specifications listed in the installation manual, including pressure, temperature, or load beyond rated limits. This falls under the client's own operational responsibility.

4.5 · Unauthorized Modifications

Welding, drilling, or any structural modification made to the unit by the client or a third party, without prior written approval from Ventix, voids the warranty on the affected section, regardless of whether the modification is related to the eventual failure.

4.6 · Oil Sample Requirement

At the time of any failure, an oil sample must be collected and submitted before the failed part is discarded. A claim without a sample can still meet the conditions for approval, but only if the client can show that collecting a sample was truly impossible.

4.7 · Client Ordering & Configuration Errors

Requests to return a correctly delivered unit due to incorrect model selection, undersized capacity, or changed requirements on the client's part are not covered. These situations fall outside our responsibility, not Ventix's.

4.8 · Ownership Transfer & Registration

Warranty coverage is tied to the end user on file at the time of purchase. If a unit moves to another facility or owner without updating the registration, any new claim may be delayed until Ventix re-verifies ownership.

Teacher's Note
"Void" and "avoid" look and sound almost identical to Portuguese speakers, but they're unrelated. "Void" means to cancel or invalidate something (anular). "Avoid" means to stay away from something (evitar). "The modification voids the warranty" has nothing to do with avoiding it. "Void" e "avoid" parecem quase a mesma palavra, mas não têm relação nenhuma. "Void" significa anular algo. "Avoid" significa evitar algo.
"Waive" and "wave" sound exactly the same but mean completely different things. To "waive" a requirement means to officially give it up or not enforce it ("Ventix waived the oil sample requirement in this case"). To "wave" is just to move your hand as a greeting. "Waive" e "wave" soam idênticos, mas "waive" significa dispensar ou abrir mão de uma exigência, enquanto "wave" é simplesmente acenar.
Mission: try building your own sentence with each verb, one with "void" (not "avoid"), one with "waive" (not "wave"). Say them out loud before moving to the cases below.
