Questions you can use in any conversation. Every question uses the verb patterns from the lesson. Pick a few, practice with your buddy, and make the structure automatic.

How to use this page

Pick 3 questions from any tab. Ask your buddy. Listen to their answer. Then switch: they ask, you answer. The goal is to say the question out loud, naturally, without thinking about the grammar. If you need help, tap the Portuguese translation below each question.
