EXHIBIT B
Conditions Applicable to Service Plans
Attached to and forming part of Service Agreement SA-4471 · Rev. 04 · Page 1 of 3

Where this Exhibit conflicts with the Quotation, the Quotation takes precedence.

B.1
Scope of Each Visit

The Quotation states the number of visits per year and the activities to be carried out at each visit. Work that is not on that activity list is quoted separately. After each visit Ventix issues a service report, which must be signed off by a representative of the client. Equipment not named in the Quotation is outside the scope of this Agreement.

B.2
Repairs, Consumables and Standby Air

This Agreement covers inspection and scheduled maintenance. Repairs identified during a visit are recorded in the service report as a recommendation and quoted separately, except where the client holds the Total Responsibility Plan. Operating materials, consumables and spare parts are billed separately as required. Standby air, including the temporary hire of a replacement unit, is not included unless agreed in writing.

B.3
Working Hours, Access and Waiting Time

Visits are carried out during normal working hours, Monday to Friday, 07:30 to 16:00, excluding public holidays. Work performed outside those hours is charged at the overtime rate in force on the date of the visit. Where the technician waits more than thirty minutes for access to the unit, additional hours may be charged. Where access is refused and the visit has to be rescheduled, travel time and mileage are borne by the client.

B.4
Price Adjustments

The annual price is reviewed on completion of each twelve months from the commencement date (the review date). Regardless of the term, and even where the price is stated as fixed, the price is subject to adjustment at any time if a major change occurs in the operating or site conditions of the unit. Major changes include moving the unit, even within the same facility; positioning other equipment so that cooling air intake is affected; electrical changes; and exceeding the estimated annual running hours stated in the Quotation by more than one thousand hours. The price is also subject to adjustment on any addition of service.

B.5
Client Responsibilities

Under all Service Plans the client shall carry out the daily and weekly inspections set out in the instruction manual; use only genuine Ventix parts and approved lubricants; keep the unit within the environmental conditions recommended by Ventix; notify Ventix immediately of any change in operating or site conditions; and provide free and full access to the unit, together with adequate lighting, power and any lifting equipment required, at the client's own cost.

B.6
When Ventix Is Not Obliged to Service

Ventix shall not be obliged to inspect or service the unit where the client has failed to perform its responsibilities under B.5; where a failure results from accidental or wilful damage by the client or a third party, from interruption or fluctuation of electric power, or from introduced contamination; or where the unit has been operated outside specified parameters. In such cases Ventix may still attend the site, and the work is quoted as a repair.

B.7
Overhauls

Where monitoring readings taken by Ventix indicate that an overhaul of the element or main motor is required, the client shall make the unit available. If the client fails to do so, Ventix's obligation to service that unit under this Agreement ceases. The overhaul is quoted separately. Where the Agreement is terminated early after an overhaul has been performed, the client shall pay the list price of the overhaul.

B.8
Term, Renewal and Termination

The initial term is stated in the Quotation. The Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives written notice at least thirty days before the end of the current term. Either party may terminate on thirty days' written notice. Ventix may suspend or terminate with immediate effect where a major change occurs in the operating or site conditions, or where the client commits a continuing breach that is not remedied within thirty days of written notice. On termination the client is entitled to a refund for services already paid for but not performed.

Teacher's Note · Two Words Brazilians Get Wrong Here

quote and budget. Um quote é o preço que você manda para o cliente. Um budget é o dinheiro que a empresa reservou para gastar. "I'll send you a budget" soa como se você fosse mandar o orçamento interno da sua própria empresa. Orçamento que você envia = quote / quotation. Orçamento que você tem para gastar = budget.

schedule and program. Em inglês você schedules uma visita, uma reunião, um overhaul. Program é o que você faz com uma máquina ou um software. Programar uma visita = schedule a visit. Programar o controlador = program the controller.

02
Ten Calls, Three Answers

Toda ligação cai numa de três caixas. Included está no plano e não gera fatura. Billable a Ventix faz, mas cobra. Not covered não é responsabilidade da Ventix. A diferença entre as duas últimas é o que o cliente mais confunde, e é o que você precisa saber dizer.

Careful with this argument. O overhaul acontece uma vez a cada vários anos. Num ano sem overhaul, o Northwind pagaria 12.080 dólares e o Service Plan sairia mais barato. O argumento honesto do Alex não é "o outro plano é sempre mais barato", é "você não sabe em qual ano você está". Se o aluno usar o número sem essa ressalva, o David Kim derruba na primeira reunião.
