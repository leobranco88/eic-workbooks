Self-Development · Before We Start

Before the module, a few honest questions.

No case study yet. No Rennan, no Isabela, no Marina. Just four questions about how you actually think about growth, feedback, ambition, and success, before you see how someone else handled them.

Start here
01 · Comfort Zone vs. Growth

What actually makes a risk worth taking?

Not whether you'd take it. What separates a risk worth taking from one that isn't.

01
What makes a risk calculated instead of just reckless?
Think about the difference in general, not about one specific decision.
02
What is the difference between confidence and actually being ready?
Can someone be ready without feeling confident? Can they feel confident without being ready?
03
When does discomfort mean you're growing, and when does it just mean the fit is wrong?
Both can feel identical in the moment. What tells them apart?
04
What does imposter syndrome actually protect someone from, if anything?
Is it purely negative, or does it serve some function?
02 · Feedback Culture

Why is honest feedback so hard to give well?

Almost everyone agrees feedback should be honest. Almost no one agrees on what that requires in practice.

05
What is the difference between being blunt and being constructive?
Both can be equally true. What separates them?
06
What makes feedback land as helpful instead of as an attack, regardless of how true it is?
Think about the shape of the message, not just its content.
07
When does sugarcoating protect a relationship, and when does it just protect the person giving the feedback?
Whose comfort is actually being protected in each case?
08
Can someone be both kind and completely honest at the same time, or is that always a compromise?
Argue it either way before you decide.
03 · Ambition vs. Contentment

Contentment and complacency feel identical.

Both sound like "I'm happy where I am." The module will ask you to tell them apart in someone else's career. Start with the concept itself.

01
The core question
What is the actual difference between contentment and complacency?
They can look exactly the same from the outside. What would you look for?
02
The trap
What makes ambition healthy instead of just restlessness?
Both want something different. What's missing from restlessness that healthy ambition has?
03
The harder question
Is it possible to know, in the moment, which one you're actually experiencing?
Or is this only visible in hindsight?
Your reflection
Think about your own career in general terms, not a specific decision. Right now, would you call yourself content, ambitious, restless, or complacent? What makes you say that?
04 · Success on Your Own Terms

Who actually defines success for you?

These questions don't have a right answer. They're about how you personally think about the word "success," before the module offers its own framing.

09
What makes a definition of success actually someone's own, instead of inherited from a parent, a boss, or a culture?
Is an inherited definition automatically wrong, or can it still be genuinely yours?
10
Why do people find it so hard to define success without mentioning money or title?
What does that difficulty actually reveal?
Before we start
In two or three sentences, and without using the words "money" or "position": what would you personally call a successful career?
