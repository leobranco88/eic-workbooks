Before opening the
15 Student Profiles

Facilitator script
~15 min · short version 5 min
1Warm-up opener
2 min
What to say
"Raise your hand if, in the last two weeks, one single student hijacked an entire lesson."
2Name Your Challenge
3 to 4 min · individual, with post-its
What to say
"Grab 3 post-its. On each one, write down one student behavior that challenges you the most right now, one per post-it. Just describe what happens, no need to name a type. Stick all 3 inside the boxes on your worksheet. We'll come back to them later."
3Which student were you?
3 to 4 min · individual
What to say
"Thinking back to your own school days, do you recognize yourself in any behavior we're covering today?"
4Bridge into the material
2 min
What to say
"Today we're not handing you a list of difficult students. We're giving you a vocabulary. By the end, some students who today only exist in your head as 'that annoying one' will have a name, a known trap, and a ready plan. Guess: how many profiles do you think we mapped?"
If time runs short

5-minute version

Just blocks 1 and 4, cutting the two middle activities. The warm-up opener sets the mood, the closing bridge already delivers the hook, and the room moves straight into the material.
