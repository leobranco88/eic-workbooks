Before you start
None of this is about you. A student who shuts down, tests limits, or lashes out isn't reading your teaching ability, they're responding to something else entirely: fear of failure, a rough week at home, a normal developmental stage, or, sometimes, something that genuinely needs adult intervention. Use this guide, adjust what's in your control, and leave the rest at the classroom door. Your worth as a teacher was never riding on any single student's hard day.
