03 Gerund Check 12 items

After a preposition, the verb always takes the -ING form. No exceptions.

Watch out: "committed to doing", "object to doing", "used to doing" — the "to" here is a preposition, not an infinitive marker. Never write "committed to do".
verb bank
accept · carry out · check · escalate · flag · meet · pay · process · reject · sign · store · submit
Verbs are in base form and NOT in order.
1.
Carolina insisted on submitting an accredited lab report before resubmitting the claim.
2.
The payment terms prevented Althea from signing any backdated agreement with Hua Li.
3.
Kevin objected to accepting full liability for the concentration issue.
4.
Althea is committed to carrying out a monthly invoice verification process from now on.
5.
The warehouse team is responsible for checking every delivery on arrival.
6.
Carolina succeeded in flagging the short delivery within the 3-day window.
7.
Hua Li insisted on processing the undisputed portion of the invoice immediately.
8.
The finance director is not used to signing off on invoices with open disputes.
9.
Both legal teams succeeded in meeting within the agreed 30-day window.
10.
Carolina is thinking about escalating the matter to arbitration if no response arrives this week.
11.
Hua Li's legal team objected to rejecting the credit note proposal outright.
12.
The supplier apologised for storing the drums incorrectly at the distribution centre.
04 Ask the Question 8 items

Turn each statement into a question. Keep the preposition at the end — that is the natural position in English.

Example: "Carolina is responsible for checking deliveries." → "What is Carolina responsible for?"
